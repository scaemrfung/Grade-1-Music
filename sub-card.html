For the substitute

Sub card: 20 minutes of Grade 1 music

No planning needed. One hand drum (or a table to tap). Works in any week of the year.

The plan

  1. 0–2 · Hello. Tap a slow, steady drum pulse. Sing or say “Hello, Grade One” on two notes (high–low). The class echoes.
  2. 2–4 · Four voices. Say “good morning” in a whisper, a talking voice, a calling voice, then sing it. Name each voice. The class copies. (Calling voice only once!)
  3. 4–8 · Engine, Engine. Chant “Engine, engine, number nine, going down Chicago line” while everyone pats the pulse. Then walk the pulse in a circle and freeze on the last word, “line.” Try it three times.
  4. 8–12 · High and low. Tap the middle of the drum (low): everyone crouches. Tap the rim or a desk (high): everyone stretches tall. Mix them up. Let two children lead.
  5. 12–18 · See-Saw. Sing “See-saw, up and down, in the air and on the ground” (high–low–high–low). Arms go up and down with the tune. Then partners hold hands and seesaw gently.
  6. 18–20 · Line up on the pulse. Tap the drum. One table at a time walks to the door on the beat and freezes.

Class rules the children know

  • Quiet hand: raise your hand; they stop and freeze. Don’t talk over them.
  • Singing voice, not a shout. Praise light voices, never the loudest.
  • If a game gets wild: stop, freeze, restart slower. It is fine to repeat Engine, Engine.
