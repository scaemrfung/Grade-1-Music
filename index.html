Alberta Grade 1 · GAMEPLAN companion

Weekly music lessons.

Thirty-six weekly lessons, each taught in three 30-minute classes (devotional song + 20 minutes of music). Folk songs with playable scores. Aligned to Alberta Grade 1 Music and sequenced in the same month-by-month order as the GAMEPLAN Grade 1 year — voices and pulse, ta and rest, so–mi, ti-ti and la, form and meter — without copying the book.

Nine units, month by month

01Weeks 1 – 4 · Sept

Voices & Pulse

Music-room routines and hello songs, pulse in the body and as a group, high/low and up/down, and first mallet manners.

02Weeks 5 – 8 · Oct

Sound, Silence & Timbre

Sound and silence with the Muffin Man, walking and jogging beats, whisper and shout voices, a steady two-note accompaniment on bars, and musical sentences felt in movement.

03Weeks 9 – 12 · Nov

Ta, Tempo & Contour

The pulse becomes ta, ta and rest rows, fast and slow, and melody shape.

04Weeks 13 – 16 · Dec

Introduction, Phrase & Loud/Quiet

Song doorways (introductions), a seasonal pulse, phrases felt, and piano/forte.

05Weeks 17 – 20 · Jan

Mallets, Bordun & Reading

Mallet manners, a two-drum bordun, so–mi on the staff, and four-beat reading.

06Weeks 21 – 24 · Feb

Ti-ti, La & Phrases

Ti-ti felt and read, la joins so and mi, and phrases named with call and response.

07Weeks 25 – 28 · Mar

Body Levels, AB, Meter & Ostinato

Snap, clap, pat, stamp; AB form; marching in 2 and swaying in 4; speech ostinato.

08Weeks 29 – 32 · Apr

Pitched Timbre, Repeat, ABA & 6/8

Pitched wood and metal, the repeat sign, ABA with a composed B, and the 6/8 swing.

09Weeks 33 – 36 · May–June

Create, Share, Celebrate

Student four-beat pieces, an in-class concertette, picture checks, requests and listening.

All 36 weeks

Week 1 · Welcome to the Music Room · Aug 31–Sept 4 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — We learn how our Music room works: where we sit on the floor, our three rules, the quiet hand, and how to move to a steady pulse. We meet the hello song, our first instruments, and a freeze game.Week 2 · Pulse in the Body · Sept 8–11 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — Music has a steady pulse, like a heartbeat. We feel it by patting, walking and leading it, we change the speed, and we notice what stays the same and what is different.Week 3 · Pulse as a Group · Sept 14–18 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — We keep one pulse together as a class: drawing it as hearts, playing it on woodblocks under a rhyme, and following a leader. We also move high and low and sing our names on so–mi.Week 4 · High, Low, Up and Down · Sept 21–25 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — Sounds can be high or low, and a melody can go up or down. We move up and down with our bodies and voices, then play it with the mallet on a xylophone or glockenspiel.Week 5 · Sound and Silence · Sept 28–Oct 1 · Sound, Silence & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — Music has sounds and silences. In The Muffin Man every muffin is a sound and an empty plate is a silent beat: we keep the pulse going inside. We meet spider songs, puppets, and our first gentle mallet turns.Week 6 · Silent Beats and Walking and Jogging Beats · Oct 5–9 · Sound, Silence & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — We keep the pulse through silence with the Muffin Man, feel walking beats and jogging beats in Big Black Cats, and find up and down in Eency Weency Spider.Week 7 · Loud and Quiet Voices, Beats and Bars · Oct 13–16 · Sound, Silence & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — We use whisper and strong voices in Stirring My Brew, step walking and jogging beats on paper sheets, play Big Black Cats on woodblocks and glockenspiel, and hold a steady two-note accompaniment under Candy Store.Week 8 · Musical Sentences and the Mountain King · Oct 19–23 · Sound, Silence & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — Songs and poems are made of musical sentences. We feel them in In the Hall of the Mountain King, count them in Little Jack Pumpkin Face, and play a steady two-note accompaniment under What Will You Be?Week 9 · Ta: The Pulse Has a Name · Oct 26–30 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — The heartbeat pulse now gets a name: ta. We say ta, clap ta, and read hearts as ta.Week 10 · Ta and Rest · Nov 2–6 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — We read and play ta and rest. A rest is a silent beat we can see on a card.Week 11 · Fast and Slow · Nov 16–20 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — Music can go fast or slow. That speed is called tempo. The pulse can change speed but it still stays steady.Week 12 · Melody Shape: Contour · Nov 23–27 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — A melody has a shape. It goes up, down, or stays the same. We draw it in the air and on the board.Week 13 · The Introduction (Song Doorway) · Nov 30–Dec 4 · Introduction, Phrase & Loud/Quiet · 3 × 30 min — Many songs start with an introduction: a little doorway before the singing starts. We listen, wait, and come in together.Week 14 · Seasonal Pulse · Dec 7–11 · Introduction, Phrase & Loud/Quiet · 3 × 30 min — We keep the pulse in a seasonal song: the Jingle Bells chorus, with jingles and steps.Week 15 · Feel the Phrase · Jan 4–8 · Introduction, Phrase & Loud/Quiet · 3 × 30 min — Songs are made of phrases, like sentences. We feel each phrase with a rainbow arm and a paper plate.Week 16 · Quiet and Loud · Jan 11–15 · Introduction, Phrase & Loud/Quiet · 3 × 30 min — Music can be quiet (piano) or loud (forte). We choose mouse or lion and control our sound.Week 17 · Mallet Manners · Jan 18–22 · Mallets, Bordun & Reading · 3 × 30 min — Mallets need manners. Rest, Ready, Play, Freeze: we learn to hold a mallet and let it bounce off the bar.Week 18 · Bordun and Pulse · Jan 25–28 · Mallets, Bordun & Reading · 3 × 30 min — A bordun is a steady low pattern under a song. We make one with two drums playing together on the pulse.Week 19 · So and Mi Get Names · Feb 8–12 · Mallets, Bordun & Reading · 3 × 30 min — High and low notes now have names: so and mi. We sign them, sing them, and see them on a three-line staff.Week 20 · Reading Four Beats · Feb 16–19 · Mallets, Bordun & Reading · 3 × 30 min — The heart rows become rhythm rows. We read four-beat rows of ta and rest.Week 21 · Ti-ti: Two Sounds on a Beat · Feb 22–26 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — Some beats have two short sounds. We feel them in names and words before we write them.Week 22 · Ti-ti and Ta · Mar 1–4 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — We read and play ti-ti and ta in songs like Lucy Locket and Star Light.Week 23 · Add La · Mar 8–12 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — A new note joins so and mi: la, just above so. We sign it and sing it.Week 24 · Phrases Have Names · Mar 15–18 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — A phrase is a musical sentence. We name phrases, mirror them with a partner, and try call and response.Week 25 · Four Levels of Body Percussion · Mar 30–Apr 2 · Body Levels, AB, Meter & Ostinato · 3 × 30 min — Our bodies have four sound levels: snap, clap, pat and stamp. We play patterns that move between them.Week 26 · AB: Two Different Parts · Apr 5–9 · Body Levels, AB, Meter & Ostinato · 3 × 30 min — Same/different gets a name: A and B. A song or piece can have two different parts.Week 27 · Marching in 2, Swaying in 4 · Apr 12–16 · Body Levels, AB, Meter & Ostinato · 3 × 30 min — Beats come in groups. We march in 2 and sway in 4 with Jim Along Josie and Punchinella.Week 28 · Ostinato: A Pattern That Repeats · Apr 19–23 · Body Levels, AB, Meter & Ostinato · 3 × 30 min — An ostinato is a short pattern that repeats under a song. We start with speech ostinatos.Week 29 · Pitched Wood and Metal · Apr 26–30 · Pitched Timbre, Repeat, ABA & 6/8 · 3 × 30 min — Some instruments play different pitches. Xylophones are wood; glockenspiels are metal. We hear the difference and match it to woodblock and triangle.Week 30 · The Repeat Sign · May 3–6 · Pitched Timbre, Repeat, ABA & 6/8 · 3 × 30 min — Two dots and two lines mean “do it again.” We find repeat signs and play a part twice.Week 31 · ABA: Come Back to A · May 10–14 · Pitched Timbre, Repeat, ABA & 6/8 · 3 × 30 min — A piece can go A, then B, then back to A. We compose our own B with ta and rest.Week 32 · The 6/8 Swing · May 17–19 · Pitched Timbre, Repeat, ABA & 6/8 · 3 × 30 min — Some songs swing in two big beats, each with three little ones. We feel 6/8 in Hickory Dickory Dock and Jack and Jill.Week 33 · My Four-Beat Piece · May 25–28 · Create, Share, Celebrate · 3 × 30 min — Each student makes a short four-beat piece with ta, ti-ti and rest, and plays it.Week 34 · Our Concertette · May 31–June 4 · Create, Share, Celebrate · 3 × 30 min — We share a four-minute concertette in class and practise being a good audience. O Canada closes.Week 35 · Picture Check Stations · June 7–11 · Create, Share, Celebrate · 3 × 30 min — We show what we know at five picture-check stations: pulse, rest, so–mi, mallet bounce and AB.Week 36 · Requests and Listening · June 14–18 · Create, Share, Celebrate · 3 × 30 min — We choose favourite songs and games, and listen with respect to music of First Nations, Métis and Inuit peoples.
