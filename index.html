Alberta Grade 1 · GAMEPLAN companion

Weekly music lessons.

Thirty-six weekly lessons, each taught in three 30-minute classes (devotional song + 20 minutes of music). Folk songs with playable scores. Aligned to Alberta Grade 1 Music and sequenced in the same month-by-month order as the GAMEPLAN Grade 1 year — voices and pulse, ta and rest, so–mi, ti-ti and la, form and meter — without copying the book.

Nine units, month by month

01Weeks 1 – 4 · Sept

Voices & Pulse

Music-room routines and hello songs, pulse in the body and as a group, high/low and up/down, and first mallet manners.

02Weeks 5 – 8 · Oct

Sound and Silence

Sound and silence with the Muffin Man, walking and jogging beats, whisper and shout voices, a steady two-note accompaniment on bars, and musical sentences felt in movement.

03Weeks 9 – 12 · Nov

Ta, Tempo & Contour

The pulse becomes ta, ta and rest rows, fast and slow, and melody shape.

04Weeks 13 – 15 · Dec

Introduction & Phrase

Phrase echo and holiday pulse, toy rhythms and marionettes, and high/low phrases with seasonal songs.

05Weeks 16 – 19 · Jan

So–Mi, Notes & Reading

Label so–mi and hand signs, so–mi on bars and staff, label quarter and eighth notes, winter singing games, rhythm cards and The Kangaroo.

06Weeks 20 – 23 · Feb

Ti-ti, La & Phrases

Ti-ti on rhythm cards, so–mi staff and Valentine, Drummer Hoff ostinato and patriotic songs, then label la with Bounce High, light mallets, and unpitched listening.

07Weeks 24 – 27 · Mar

SML, Dynamics, ABA & Timbre

So–mi–la and dynamics with hunting instruments, notate la and label ABA with A Kite, Jack and Jill contour with wood/metal/skin families, then Acka Backa and quiet bunnies.

08Weeks 28 – 31 · Apr

Ostinato, ABA & Pitched Percussion

Hammer ABA and rain ostinato with Village, Sugar Sugar wood/metal and Tres Pajaritos, Caboose trains with Drip Drop, then Buttercup instruments, Higglety ostinato, and the p.94 She’ll be Comin’ / Caboose finish.

09Weeks 32 – 36 · May–June

SML Check, Ostinato & Singing Games

All About Success and SML assessment with Mexican Counting Song and Allison’s Camel, Bell Horses and Punchinella, Gallant Ship timbres and Jump Jim Joe, then Otto speech phrases, I Lost My Glove, Willum improv, year-end game review, and a light June requests and listening week that reuses favourites already taught.

All 36 weeks

Week 1 · Welcome to the Music Room · Aug 31–Sept 4 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — We learn how our Music room works: where we sit on the floor, our three rules, the quiet hand, and how to move to a steady pulse. We meet the hello song, our first instruments, and a freeze game.Week 2 · Pulse in the Body · Sept 8–11 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — Music has a steady pulse, like a heartbeat. We feel it by patting, walking and leading it, we change the speed, and we notice what stays the same and what is different.Week 3 · Pulse as a Group · Sept 14–18 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — We keep one pulse together as a class: drawing it as hearts, playing it on woodblocks under a rhyme, and following a leader. We also move high and low and sing our names on so–mi.Week 4 · High, Low, Up and Down · Sept 21–25 · Voices & Pulse · 3 × 30 min — Sounds can be high or low, and a melody can go up or down. We move up and down with our bodies and voices, then play it with the mallet on a xylophone or glockenspiel.Week 5 · Sound and Silence · Sept 28–Oct 1 · Sound and Silence · 3 × 30 min — Music has sounds and silences. In The Muffin Man every muffin is a sound and an empty plate is a silent beat: we keep the pulse going inside. We meet spider songs, puppets, and our first gentle mallet turns.Week 6 · Silent Beats and Walking and Jogging Beats · Oct 5–9 · Sound and Silence · 3 × 30 min — We keep the pulse through silence with the Muffin Man, feel walking beats and jogging beats in Big Black Cats, and find up and down in Eency Weency Spider.Week 7 · Loud and Quiet Voices, Beats and Bars · Oct 13–16 · Sound and Silence · 3 × 30 min — We use whisper and strong voices in Stirring My Brew, step walking and jogging beats on paper sheets, play Big Black Cats on woodblocks and glockenspiel, and hold a steady two-note accompaniment under Candy Store.Week 8 · Musical Sentences and the Mountain King · Oct 19–23 · Sound and Silence · 3 × 30 min — Songs and poems are made of musical sentences. We feel them in In the Hall of the Mountain King, count them in Little Jack Pumpkin Face, and play a steady two-note accompaniment under What Will You Be?Week 9 · Ta: The Pulse Has a Name · Oct 26–30 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — The heartbeat pulse now gets a name: ta. We say ta, clap ta, and keep it under Engine Engine and Yankee Doodle. We also meet a short introduction before the singing starts, and we try the pulse fast and slow.Week 10 · Ta and Rest · Nov 2–6 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — We keep ta steady and add a silent beat we can see: rest. Squirrel songs and a train chart give us practice. We also meet a repeat sign so a pattern can be spoken twice.Week 11 · Fast and Slow · Nov 16–20 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — The pulse can go fast or slow and still stay steady. We walk and freeze in Walk and Stop, wait through a whole musical sentence, pass a woodblock on the pulse, and share what we are thankful for.Week 12 · Melody Shape: Contour · Nov 23–27 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · 3 × 30 min — A melody has a shape: up, down, or the same. We draw it in the air, build ta and rest with sticks, keep the pulse in Hello Everybody, and move with Jim Along Josie.Week 13 · Phrase Echo and Holiday Pulse · Nov 30–Dec 4 · Introduction & Phrase · 3 × 30 min — We feel how long a musical phrase is by echoing four-measure patterns, sing a so–mi weekend chat, and keep a steady pulse in a December holiday song with a short introduction before the dance.Week 14 · Toys, Marionettes and Pulse · Dec 7–11 · Introduction & Phrase · 3 × 30 min — A toy theme carries the week: we move like marionettes to a famous ballet pulse, speak toy word-chains on unpitched instruments, play Jack-in-the-Box, and prepare so–mi with Teddy Bear.Week 15 · High-Low Phrases and Holiday Songs · Jan 4–8 · Introduction & Phrase · 3 × 30 min — Teddy Bear becomes a high-low chart, we learn La Piñata with body percussion, build song rhythms with sticks, and read holiday patterns while we sing seasonal songs.Week 16 · Label So–Mi and New-Year Pulse · Jan 11–15 · So–Mi, Notes & Reading · 3 × 30 min — A new-year poem keeps the pulse, gift words become a ta/ti-ti chain, Lucky Me! adds a chord bordun feel, and so and mi finally get their names with hand signs on Teddy Bear.Week 17 · So–Mi Patterns and Shape Moves · Jan 18–22 · So–Mi, Notes & Reading · 3 × 30 min — We echo phrase-length body patterns and so–mi hand-sign patterns, build ta/ti-ti on the board, move with same/different shapes, play so–so–mi on a barred instrument for Valentine, and notate so–mi on a three-line staff.Week 18 · Label Notes and Winter Games · Jan 25–28 · So–Mi, Notes & Reading · 3 × 30 min — Stick rhythms get note heads and names, Old Gray Cats match rest and jog pulses, Baker’s Hat builds a so–mi melody, and we add winter singing games with kind loud/quiet and a bounce game that prepares la.Week 19 · Rhythm Cards, Line Notes and Kangaroo · Feb 8–12 · So–Mi, Notes & Reading · 3 × 30 min — We read four-beat rhythm cards with note heads, learn line notes vs space notes with body shapes, review so–mi echoes, and play The Kangaroo with so–mi answers and gentle barred jumps.Week 20 · Which Rhythm and Notating So–Mi · Feb 16–19 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — We hear and name four-beat cards that mix ta, ti-ti, and rest, notate so and mi on a three-line staff, sing Oliver Twist with same/different shapes, add sticks to Cumberland Square, and explore villains, damsels, and heroes with Melodrama voices.Week 21 · Shapes, Valentine and I Hope! · Feb 22–26 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — We move with same/different shapes, play so–so–mi Valentine on a barred instrument, notate so–mi with partners, read I Hope! with a mail-carrier drum game, and hide Lucy Locket’s pocket with kind loud and quiet.Week 22 · Ostinato, Presidents and Patriotic Songs · Mar 1–4 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — We freeze to a march, speak a Drummer Hoff ostinato on hand drums, cheer Celebrate the Presidents! with ta and ti-ti cards, stand for O Canada, and add actions to She’ll Be Comin’ ’Round the Mountain.Week 23 · Label La, Mallets and Miss Mary Mack · Mar 8–12 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · 3 × 30 min — We review phrase-length body patterns, bounce to prepare la, give la its name and Curwen sign, copy mallet ideas in Do As I’m Doing, guess hidden unpitched instruments, and clap Miss Mary Mack with a partner pulse.Week 24 · SML, Hunting Instruments and March is Here Now · Mar 15–18 · SML, Dynamics, ABA & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — We echo so–mi–la with hand signs and the solfege ladder, review Lucy Locket loud and quiet, sing All Around the Circle with name solos, hunt and name unpitched instruments, react to loud and quiet with Mozart, and speak March is Here Now with lion and lamb dynamics.Week 25 · Notating SML, A Kite and Instrument Assessment · Mar 30–Apr 2 · SML, Dynamics, ABA & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — We read rhythm cards with ta and ti-ti, notate so–mi–la on a three-line staff, move like a kite for contour and tempo, label ABA with A Kite, and assess unpitched listening on paper.Week 26 · Jack and Jill, Contour and Instrument Families · Apr 5–9 · SML, Dynamics, ABA & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — We copy Irish Washerwoman moves, trace contour cards with voices, sing and move Jack and Jill in ABA with a chord bordun, classify unpitched instruments as wood, metal, or skin in One, Two, Three, and form shamrock or clover groups from temple-block cues.Week 27 · Acka Backa and Bunnies on the Move · Apr 12–16 · SML, Dynamics, ABA & Timbre · 3 × 30 min — We read rhythm cards that hide rests, build Acka Backa contour with movable word cards and so–mi–la, send players to a chord bordun on “you,” and begin Bunnies on the Move with quiet same/different moves. Book note: shift this week to land before Easter if needed.Week 28 · There’s Music in a Hammer, Rain and the Village · Apr 19–23 · Ostinato, ABA & Pitched Percussion · 3 × 30 min — We warm up with phrase body percussion and so–mi–la, build There’s Music in a Hammer as ABA with mallets, skip and gallop to Handel’s Country Dance, create a Rain on the Green Grass ostinato, and play Go All Around the Village.Week 29 · Sugar Sugar, Willum and Tres Pajaritos · Apr 26–30 · Ostinato, ABA & Pitched Percussion · 3 × 30 min — We read rhythm cards with rests on the shoulders, rotate through Sugar Sugar on barred instruments (wood vs metal), invent Willum verses with leaders, sing Tres Pajaritos with contour and call/response, and review Go All Around the Village.Week 30 · Little Red Caboose and Drip, Drop · May 3–6 · Ostinato, ABA & Pitched Percussion · 3 × 30 min — We form train lines with Little Red Caboose and a speech B, notate so–mi–la on a three-line staff, build Drip, Drop as ABA with flower word chains and mallets, and begin All Around the Buttercup.Week 31 · All Around the Buttercup and Higglety Pop · May 10–14 · Ostinato, ABA & Pitched Percussion · 3 × 30 min — We read rhythm cards speaking only ta while thinking ti-ti and rests, warm up so–mi–la, orchestrate All Around the Buttercup with bordun, glockenspiel, and unpitched hits, and layer Higglety, Pigglety Pop with a “Please stop eating the mop!” ostinato, then finish April with She’ll be Comin’ instruments and a Caboose body-percussion ostinato (p. 94).Week 32 · All About Success, SML Check and Mexican Counting Song · May 17–19 · SML Check, Ostinato & Singing Games · 3 × 30 min — We read rhythm cards speaking ti-ti while thinking ta and rests, sing All About Success with so–mi–la echoes, prepare Which Melody Do You Hear?, take Written Assessment #4 on so–mi–la, and learn the Mexican Counting Song with Allison’s Camel.Week 33 · Bell Horses, Counting Ostinato and Punchinella · May 25–28 · SML Check, Ostinato & Singing Games · 3 × 30 min — We build a body-percussion ostinato under the Mexican Counting Song, notate recent song rhythms with popsicle sticks, orchestrate Bell Horses with so on the middle line, and play Punchinella.Week 34 · The Gallant Ship, Rhythm Ostinatos and Jump Jim Joe · May 31–June 4 · SML Check, Ostinato & Singing Games · 3 × 30 min — We clap quarters and pat eighths on rhythm cards, play an Amos Moses ostinato listening game, orchestrate The Gallant Ship with pitched and unpitched timbres in AB, notate so–mi–la, and play Jump Jim Joe.Week 35 · Otto Goes to the Beach, I Lost My Glove and Year-End Games · June 7–11 · SML Check, Ostinato & Singing Games · 3 × 30 min — We speak a rhythm-card ostinato, build Poor Otto / Lucky Otto speech phrases from Todd Parr’s book, play I Lost My Glove, improvise on Willum with unpitched instruments, and choose favourite singing games before summer.Week 36 · Year-End Favourites and Listening · June 14–18 · SML Check, Ostinato & Singing Games · 3 × 30 min — We vote for favourite songs and games already learned this year, play student requests, listen with care to We Belong to the Drum, and celebrate what Grade 1 musicians can do before summer.
