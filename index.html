Alberta Grade 1 · GAMEPLAN companion

Weekly music lessons.

Thirty-six weekly lessons, each taught in three 30-minute classes (devotional song + 20 minutes of music). Folk songs with playable scores. Aligned to Alberta Grade 1 Music and to the public GAMEPLAN Grade 1 year — pulse, ta and rest, ti-ti, so–mi–la — without copying the book.

Six units

01Weeks 1 – 6

Voice & Echo

Speaking voice, singing voice, echo games, and so–mi.

02Weeks 7 – 12

Steady Beat

Feel, walk, and play a steady beat. Strong beats and freeze games.

03Weeks 13 – 18

Long, Short, Silence

Ta, ti-ti, and rest. Four-beat patterns a Grade 1 can clap and write with icons.

04Weeks 19 – 24

High & Low

Melody goes up and down. So–mi–la hand signs and tiny tunes.

05Weeks 25 – 30

Instruments & Colour

Body percussion, classroom instruments, found sounds, loud and quiet, fast and slow.

06Weeks 31 – 36

Songs to Share

Same and different, call and response, audience manners, and a class sharing.

All 36 weeks

Week 1 · Hello, Music Class · September · Voice & Echo · 3 × 30 min — Learn how music class works: we sing, we listen, we move, and we stop when the hand goes up.Week 2 · Talking Voice, Singing Voice · September · Voice & Echo · 3 × 30 min — Feel the difference between a speaking voice and a singing voice, and find a light singing tone.Week 3 · Echo Me · September · Voice & Echo · 3 × 30 min — Copy short claps and short sung patterns. Echo is how Grade 1 learns almost every song.Week 4 · So and Mi · September · Voice & Echo · 3 × 30 min — Two pitches: so (higher) and mi (lower). Hand signs and a tiny song.Week 5 · Singing Games · October · Voice & Echo · 3 × 30 min — A song can be a game. We sing, we move, we take turns, we keep the tune.Week 6 · We Can Sing Together · October · Voice & Echo · 3 × 30 min — Unison: one tune, many voices. This is hand-in project 1 — our class song.Week 7 · Feel the Beat · October · Steady Beat · 3 × 30 min — The beat is the steady heart of the song — even when the words are long or short.Week 8 · Walk the Beat · October · Steady Beat · 3 × 30 min — Take the steady beat into the feet. Walk, freeze, walk again.Week 9 · Hands, Feet, and Freeze · November · Steady Beat · 3 × 30 min — Switch the beat between body parts. Control is the skill.Week 10 · Strong Beats and Quiet Beats · November · Steady Beat · 3 × 30 min — Some beats feel heavier. We can show a strong beat without getting loud and wild.Week 11 · Beat vs the Words · November · Steady Beat · 3 × 30 min — The beat stays even. The rhythm is the pattern of the words. They are not the same.Week 12 · Beat Parade · November · Steady Beat · 3 × 30 min — Hand-in project 2: walk or play a steady beat, freeze on silence, stay safe.Week 13 · Long and Short · December · Long, Short, Silence · 3 × 30 min — Sounds can be long or short. We hear it, we draw it, we clap it.Week 14 · Ta · December · Long, Short, Silence · 3 × 30 min — The long sound that fills one beat has a name: ta. We say it, clap it, and see it.Week 15 · Ti-ti · December · Long, Short, Silence · 3 × 30 min — Two short sounds that share one beat: ti-ti.Week 16 · Sh — the Rest · December · Long, Short, Silence · 3 × 30 min — Silence is music too. A rest is one beat of no sound, with a still body.Week 17 · Four-Beat Patterns · January · Long, Short, Silence · 3 × 30 min — Put ta, ti-ti, and rest together. Read, clap, play.Week 18 · My Four-Beat Pattern · January · Long, Short, Silence · 3 × 30 min — Hand-in project 3: one four-beat pattern the class can echo.Week 19 · High and Low · January · High & Low · 3 × 30 min — Pitch can be high or low. We hear it, we show it with our bodies, and we sing it.Week 20 · Melody Goes Up and Down · January · High & Low · 3 × 30 min — A melody is a line. It can go up, down, or stay. We draw the line as we sing.Week 21 · So–Mi Songs · February · High & Low · 3 × 30 min — Put high and low into real songs. So–mi is the Grade 1 singing home.Week 22 · Add La · February · High & Low · 3 × 30 min — La sits above so. Three pitches: so, mi, and la. Bounce High uses them.Week 23 · Hand Signs We Can Read · February · High & Low · 3 × 30 min — The teacher’s hands are a score. We read so–mi–la without words.Week 24 · A Little Melody · February · High & Low · 3 × 30 min — Make a tiny so–mi–la tune, four beats long, and sing it for a friend.Week 25 · Body Percussion · March · Instruments & Colour · 3 × 30 min — Clap, pat, stamp, snap — four sounds we already own. Transfer the beat and simple patterns onto the body.Week 26 · Classroom Instruments with Care · March · Instruments & Colour · 3 × 30 min — Rhythm sticks, shakers, bells, drums. How we hold them, how we rest them, how we play a beat.Week 27 · Name, Sound, Picture · March · Instruments & Colour · 3 × 30 min — Match an instrument’s name, its sound, and what it looks like. Alberta Grade 1 loves this game.Week 28 · Found Sounds and New Ways · March · Instruments & Colour · 3 × 30 min — Music can come from pencils, paper, chairs (gently), and invented instruments. We explore with rules.Week 29 · Fast and Slow, Loud and Quiet · April · Instruments & Colour · 3 × 30 min — Tempo and dynamics. We change the music on purpose and still stay together.Week 30 · Listen with Respect · April · Instruments & Colour · 3 × 30 min — We listen to music of First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples, and music from many places, with care. We do not copy what is not ours to copy.Week 31 · Same and Different · April · Songs to Share · 3 × 30 min — Form we can feel: this part is the same, that part is different. Echo and verse live here.Week 32 · Call and Response · April · Songs to Share · 3 × 30 min — A leader sings, the group answers. We practise being both.Week 33 · Choose What We Will Share · May · Songs to Share · 3 × 30 min — Pick two songs and one played piece for the sharing concert. Rehearse the order.Week 34 · How to Be an Audience · May · Songs to Share · 3 × 30 min — When we are not the ones singing, we still have a job: still, kind, clap at the end.Week 35 · Sharing Concert · May · Songs to Share · 3 × 30 min — Hand-in project 4. We share two songs and one played piece with our audience.Week 36 · What I Know About Music Now · May · Songs to Share · 3 × 30 min — Remember the year: beat, ta and ti-ti, so–mi–la, instruments, listening with respect. Celebrate.
