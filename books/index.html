Picture books

Story suggestions

One book (or read-aloud) for each of the 36 weeks. Same titles and links as on the lesson pages — buy the book or watch a read-aloud, then teach the week.

How to use the books. Each lesson page has a “How to use this book” section under its story suggestion: when to read it (hook, brain break, or close, usually 5–7 minutes of one class’s 20 music minutes), what to listen for before reading, music moves during the story, and a follow-up activity with a quick check. Read-aloud video links come from author, publisher, or storytime channels. Preview them before class, and use the book itself when you can.

Weeks 1 and 36 also include a short original “Optional story” on the lesson page (not reprinted here).
