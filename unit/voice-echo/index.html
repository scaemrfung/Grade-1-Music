Unit 1

Voice & Echo

Speaking voice, singing voice, echo games, and so–mi.

Students distinguish speaking and singing voices and echo simple songs in tune.

Print this unit
Week 1 · Hello, Music Class · 3 × 30 min — Learn how music class works: we sing, we listen, we move, and we stop when the hand goes up.Week 2 · Talking Voice, Singing Voice · 3 × 30 min — Feel the difference between a speaking voice and a singing voice, and find a light singing tone.Week 3 · Echo Me · 3 × 30 min — Copy short claps and short sung patterns. Echo is how Grade 1 learns almost every song.Week 4 · So and Mi · 3 × 30 min — Two pitches: so (higher) and mi (lower). Hand signs and a tiny song.Week 5 · Singing Games · 3 × 30 min — A song can be a game. We sing, we move, we take turns, we keep the tune.Week 6 · We Can Sing Together · 3 × 30 min — Unison: one tune, many voices. This is hand-in project 1 — our class song.
