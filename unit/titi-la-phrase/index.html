Unit 6

Ti-ti, La & Phrases

Ti-ti felt and read, la joins so and mi, and phrases named with call and response.

Students read ta and ti-ti, sing so–mi–la, and name phrases.

Print this unit
Week 21 · Ti-ti: Two Sounds on a Beat · 3 × 30 min — Some beats have two short sounds. We feel them in names and words before we write them.Week 22 · Ti-ti and Ta · 3 × 30 min — We read and play ti-ti and ta in songs like Lucy Locket and Star Light.Week 23 · Add La · 3 × 30 min — A new note joins so and mi: la, just above so. We sign it and sing it.Week 24 · Phrases Have Names · 3 × 30 min — A phrase is a musical sentence. We name phrases, mirror them with a partner, and try call and response.
