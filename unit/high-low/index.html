Unit 4

High & Low

Melody goes up and down. So–mi–la hand signs and tiny tunes.

Students show high and low, and sing so–mi and so–mi–la with hand signs.

Print this unit
Week 19 · High and Low · 3 × 30 min — Pitch can be high or low. We hear it, we show it with our bodies, and we sing it.Week 20 · Melody Goes Up and Down · 3 × 30 min — A melody is a line. It can go up, down, or stay. We draw the line as we sing.Week 21 · So–Mi Songs · 3 × 30 min — Put high and low into real songs. So–mi is the Grade 1 singing home.Week 22 · Add La · 3 × 30 min — La sits above so. Three pitches: so, mi, and la. Bounce High uses them.Week 23 · Hand Signs We Can Read · 3 × 30 min — The teacher’s hands are a score. We read so–mi–la without words.Week 24 · A Little Melody · 3 × 30 min — Make a tiny so–mi–la tune, four beats long, and sing it for a friend.
