Unit 5

Instruments & Colour

Body percussion, classroom instruments, found sounds, loud and quiet, fast and slow.

Students explore instrument sounds, match name–sound–picture, and play a beat with care.

Print this unit
Week 25 · Body Percussion · 3 × 30 min — Clap, pat, stamp, snap — four sounds we already own. Transfer the beat and simple patterns onto the body.Week 26 · Classroom Instruments with Care · 3 × 30 min — Rhythm sticks, shakers, bells, drums. How we hold them, how we rest them, how we play a beat.Week 27 · Name, Sound, Picture · 3 × 30 min — Match an instrument’s name, its sound, and what it looks like. Alberta Grade 1 loves this game.Week 28 · Found Sounds and New Ways · 3 × 30 min — Music can come from pencils, paper, chairs (gently), and invented instruments. We explore with rules.Week 29 · Fast and Slow, Loud and Quiet · 3 × 30 min — Tempo and dynamics. We change the music on purpose and still stay together.Week 30 · Listen with Respect · 3 × 30 min — We listen to music of First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples, and music from many places, with care. We do not copy what is not ours to copy.
