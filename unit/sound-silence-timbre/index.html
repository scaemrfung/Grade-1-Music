Unit 2

Sound and Silence

Sound and silence with the Muffin Man, walking and jogging beats, whisper and shout voices, a steady two-note accompaniment on bars, and musical sentences felt in movement.

Students keep the pulse through silent beats, feel walking and jogging beats, use quiet and strong voices, and move to the length of a musical sentence.

Print this unit
Week 5 · Sound and Silence · 3 × 30 min — Music has sounds and silences. In The Muffin Man every muffin is a sound and an empty plate is a silent beat: we keep the pulse going inside. We meet spider songs, puppets, and our first gentle mallet turns.Week 6 · Silent Beats and Walking and Jogging Beats · 3 × 30 min — We keep the pulse through silence with the Muffin Man, feel walking beats and jogging beats in Big Black Cats, and find up and down in Eency Weency Spider.Week 7 · Loud and Quiet Voices, Beats and Bars · 3 × 30 min — We use whisper and strong voices in Stirring My Brew, step walking and jogging beats on paper sheets, play Big Black Cats on woodblocks and glockenspiel, and hold a steady two-note accompaniment under Candy Store.Week 8 · Musical Sentences and the Mountain King · 3 × 30 min — Songs and poems are made of musical sentences. We feel them in In the Hall of the Mountain King, count them in Little Jack Pumpkin Face, and play a steady two-note accompaniment under What Will You Be?
