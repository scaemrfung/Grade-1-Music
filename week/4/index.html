Week 04 · Voices & Pulse · Sept 21–25

High, Low, Up and Down

Sounds can be high or low, and a melody can go up or down. We move up and down with our bodies and voices, then play it with the mallet on a xylophone or glockenspiel.

Music room version, written for next year (2027–28) when classes are in the Music room: floor spots, instruments set out, room to move. In a homeroom (this year’s setting): sit at desks or on the carpet, bring the kit, and move in place or in the aisles.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

Sept 21–25

Full week: every Grade 1 class gets all 3 classes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. From Week 9 on, each 20 minutes has the same shape: hello on the pulse (2), voice or body (2), one idea (8), a game (6), close and line up on the pulse (2). Weeks 1–8 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Day 1 · Prepare

Bring: Glockenspiel or xylophone, mallets, hand drum, a puppet if you like

GAMEPLAN book, Day 1: p. 10

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet children on so–mi. Offer a favourite colour for the name echo.
  3. 4 minMoving up and down (book p. 10) — Voices slide up like a rocket and down like a slide, and hands and bodies follow. Then play a glissando up and down on the bars and ask “up or down?”
  4. 8 minPlaying up and down: mallet manners (book p. 10) — Show a gentle mallet hold and a relaxed bounce. Two or three children at a time play up and down the bars while the class sings a slide. Everyone gets a turn over Days 1–3.
  5. 4 minSing Me Your Name, favourite colour (book p. 10) — Pass the bean bag around the circle. The holder sings their name and a favourite colour on so–mi and the class echoes.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Drum a slow pulse and freeze at the door.

Use in class

  • 🐦High or low?Moving up and down: is the sound going up or going down?
  • 🎢Voice slidesVoice slides: follow the line up and down with your voice.

Music time: 20 min · plus 5 min devotional

Day 2 · Practice

Bring: Xylophone, glockenspiel, woodblock and mallets

GAMEPLAN book, Day 2: p. 11

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet children on so–mi.
  3. 4 minHickory, Dickory, Dock, speak (book p. 11) — Say the rhyme with a climbing hand on “up the clock” and a sliding hand on “ran down.”
  4. 8 minHickory with instruments (book p. 11) — One child plays the pulse on a woodblock. A pair plays the tune on so and mi bars on the xylophone. The glockenspiel slides up on “up the clock” and down on “ran down.” Tiptoe around the circle while players swap.
  5. 4 minMallet manners — Between players, name the three ways to treat a mallet: hold it, rest it, and give it back. Practise rest position.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Tiptoe to the door.

Use in class

  • 🪜Solfège staircaseHickory with instruments: climb the solfège stairs up and slide down.
  • 🌈Xylophone & bellsMallet manners: a screen xylophone to preview the so and mi bars.

Day 3 · Transfer

Bring: Hand drum, glockenspiel and mallets, heart cards

GAMEPLAN book, Day 3: p. 12

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet children on so–mi.
  3. 4 minMoving with style (book p. 12) — Move to the pulse in different styles: giant, floppy, tiny, tiptoe. Stop the drum to freeze.
  4. 6 minSaw, Saw, Saw Away + 10-second checks (book p. 12) + 10-second checks — Say the saw rhyme from the book with a sawing motion on each beat. Children invent a new verse. Give each child a short solo. Rotating check, group D (about 7 children): while the game runs, give each child a 10-second solo: keep eight even pats on their knees while the drum fades. Mark Y or N on the record sheet.
  5. 4 minRead-aloud · Rain, Rain, Go Away! — Use the book guide. Sing the verses and show high and low with hands. Take a moment to glissando up and down on the glockenspiel at the end.
  6. 2 minHeart rows — Lay out a row of eight hearts and point to one on every pulse. We count them. The name for a beat arrives in November.
  7. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Walk and freeze at the door.

Use in class

  • 🩺HeartbeatHeart rows: pat along and count the hearts.
  • 🐱Copycat songsSee-Saw: echo it phrase by phrase, hands moving up and down.

Story suggestion

Rain, Rain, Go Away! (Sing and Read Storybook) · illustrated by Viviana Garofoli

Nursery song commonly taught as a classic so–mi piece for Grade 1.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (The Bookworms on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Day 3, in the middle of the class (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “This whole book is a song. Listen for the words ‘rain, rain’. Which word is higher?”
  • Review the signs: mi is a flat hand, palm down, at the chest; so is a flat hand, palm facing you, thumb on top, higher at the chin.

During reading

  • Sing each page on the Rain, Rain tune. On “rain, rain,” hands go high on the first word and lower on the second.
  • Use the changing verses to keep interest, and let the class suggest a new one.

After reading

Sing one verse again. One child is the hand teacher while the class sings and shows high and low.

Quick check: “Show me the higher sound. Show me the lower sound.”

Adapt: Children who are not ready to sign can point up for so and down for mi. Everyone can sing just “rain, rain” if the full verse is too much.

Extend: Make up a new verse with a class name or a weekday (“come again on Monday”).

Teacher notes

  • Two pitched instruments at most: one glockenspiel and one xylophone.
  • Keep other children on body percussion or singing while a pair plays.
  • The beat has no name yet; “ta” comes in Week 9.
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group D (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Letting mallet players bang the bars.
  • Handing everyone an instrument at once.

Challenge

Play a glissando up and then down for the class to name.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Hickory Dickory Dock

English nursery rhyme · public domain · feel in 6/8 (swinging) · 6/8

Swing the phrase. Do not force 6/8 counting.

Game: Arms swing like a pendulum. Freeze at one o’clock.

Grade 1 experiences 6/8. They do not name it. The body learns the swing.

See-Saw

Traditional · public domain (also sung as Saw, Saw, Saw Away) · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Partners hold hands and bend on so, stretch on mi — or the reverse, as long as the class agrees.

A small knee bend on each so keeps the beat honest.
