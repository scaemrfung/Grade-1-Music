This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Each 20 minutes has the same shape: hello on the pulse (2), voice or body (2), one idea (8), a game (6), close and line up on the pulse (2). Planned for a homeroom: carpet circle or beside desks, pitches from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and a short “Bring” list for each class.

Story suggestion

Polar Bear, Polar Bear, What Do You Hear? · Bill Martin Jr. (illus. Eric Carle)

Question–answer animal sounds are a natural call-and-response pattern for Grade 1.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (The World of Eric Carle on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Day 2, in the idea slot (about 6 min).

Before reading

  • “Every page has a question and an answer. The question is the call and the answer is the response.”
  • “The response waits for the call to finish.”

During reading

  • Teacher sings the call on so–mi (“Polar Bear, Polar Bear, what do you hear?”). The class chants the response (“I hear a lion roaring in my ear”).
  • Give each animal sound its own small colour: hippo snorting (low voices), flamingo fluting (high “oo”), boa hissing (a whisper), elephant trumpeting, walrus bellowing.
  • Switch jobs: a student leader sings the call and the class responds.
  • At the end, the children make all the animal sounds. Hold a quiet hand to stop the chaos. That’s part of the job.

After reading

Each call is one phrase: draw a rainbow in the air for each call and each answer.

Quick check: “How many rainbows did we draw?”

Adapt: The repeated pattern works well for English learners. Offer animal picture cards to shy leaders.

Extend: Make a class version with instruments: “Drum, drum, what do you hear? I hear a shaker shaking in my ear.”

Teacher notes

  • A phrase is a musical sentence; don’t name “A/B” yet.
  • Plates: slide, never throw.
  • Keep phrases in songs children already know.
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group C (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Too many new songs this week.
  • Plates flying.

Challenge

Sing one phrase alone for your partner.
