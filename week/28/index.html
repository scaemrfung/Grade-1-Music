Your jobs today

Warm-up · Paper orchestra

Each child has one paper. Explore: tap, wave, rip a tiny corner if allowed. Freeze. Which sound was your favourite?

Teach

Found sound has rules

We do not wreck school things. We do not use friends as drums. We try, we share, we put it back.

Same object, new way

A pencil can tap, scrape, or roll. That is “new ways” — Alberta language, kid-sized.

Build a tiny piece

Four beats of found sound as an ostinato under a known chant.

Invented instrument

Two objects together (bottle + pencil). Name it. Play a beat. Bow.

Story suggestion

Max Found Two Sticks · Brian Pinkney

Max turns found sticks and household objects into drums—invented/found instruments.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (The Cleveland Orchestra on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Hook at the start of Teach (about 6 min). The class knows this book from week 12.

Before reading

  • “Max makes music from things that aren’t instruments. Listen for each object.”
  • “Found sounds have rules: we don’t wreck things, and we don’t use friends as drums.”

During reading

  • Before each page, ask “What will Max drum on next?” Then read: his thighs, Grandpa’s bucket, the hatboxes, the soda bottles, the garbage cans.
  • For each one, a child tries a safe classroom version (paper, an empty box, a plastic cup, a pencil on a book) and plays four beats.
  • Try one object new ways: tap, scrape, roll. Link it to “same object, new way.”
  • Keep a steady beat under the whole reading so the objects become an ostinato.

After reading

Composer pairs: pick two found sounds, write a four-beat idea with icons, and perform it once.

Quick check: “What makes something a found sound? What’s one rule for using it?”

Adapt: Give each child a sound card to hold up. Children who need movement can be “object collectors” and put things back.

Extend: Invent an instrument from two objects. Name it and play Max’s bucket rhythm on it.

Activity · Composer pairs

Pairs pick two found sounds, write a four-beat idea (icons), perform once.

Close

Reset the room better than we found it. That is still musicianship. Stamp if exploration stayed inside the rules.

Materials

  • Paper, pencils, a metal water bottle, a box
  • A “no” list: bodies, other people’s things, anything that can break

Teacher notes

  • If admin is nervous, skip rips and only tap.
  • This week makes a good photo for families if your school allows.

Watch-outs

  • Dumping a junk box with no rules. Rules first, then wonder.

Challenge

Your invented instrument plays only on the rests of a class pattern.
