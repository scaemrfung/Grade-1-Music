This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi–la

Greet on so–mi–la.

Teach

Rhythmic + melodic warm-ups · There’s Music in a Hammer (book pp. 83–84)

Phrase body percussion (review idea from p. 38): patterns that fill four measures in 2/4, building from one body level toward four. Echo so–mi–la with hand signs and a solfege ladder that shows only S, M, and L. There’s Music in a Hammer by title and page only — echo with the ladder; build a four-beat hammer/nail speech pattern as a B; perform ABA with a chord bordun if you have one; optional meows as a coda. Homeroom: one barred instrument; waiting students pat the bordun on laps.

Country Dance · Rain on the Green Grass (book pp. 84–85)

Skip vs gallop: demonstrate, name the difference (gallop leads with the same foot; skip alternates), practise with a hand drum. Listen to Handel’s Country Dance (Water Music); discover ABA; assign skip/gallop/skip; two groups move on A or B. Rain on the Green Grass (traditional): speak the rhyme; arrange four body-percussion circles as an ostinato (one circle per beat; start on one or two levels); student composers rearrange. Homeroom: skip/gallop in a desk aisle; ostinato circles on the board.

Go All Around the Village (book p. 85)

Traditional three-verse singing game: circle with hands joined, one leader outside. Vs. 1 skip around; vs. 2 windows (joined hands up) while the leader weaves; vs. 3 class claps the pulse while the leader marches and stops behind a new leader. Cumulative — add a leader each repeat. Homeroom: smaller circle beside desks if space is tight.

How to use this book

When: Day 2, before Rain on the Green Grass (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Tess wants rain. Show me rain fingers on your lap.”
  • Soft finger rain on a steady pulse.

During reading

  • Finger-rain on hot pages; freeze when the first drops come.
  • Whisper “come on, rain” once together.

After reading

Speak one rain word on the pulse (drip, drop, splash).

Quick check: “Was our rain loud or quiet?”

Adapt: Seated finger rain only.

Extend: Add a four-beat rain ostinato under the last page.

Activity · Read-aloud · Come On, Rain!

Use the book guide. Quiet rain fingers.

Close

Pat four ostinato beats, then walk.
