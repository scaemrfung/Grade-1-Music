Week 34 · SML Check, Ostinato & Singing Games · May 31–June 4

The Gallant Ship, Rhythm Ostinatos and Jump Jim Joe

We clap quarters and pat eighths on rhythm cards, play an Amos Moses ostinato listening game, orchestrate The Gallant Ship with pitched and unpitched timbres in AB, notate so–mi–la, and play Jump Jim Joe.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

May 31–June 4

Full week: every Grade 1 class gets all 3 classes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi–la

Greet on so–mi–la.

Teach

Rhythm cards · Amos Moses game (book p. 101)

Rhythm cards: clap quarters, pat (alternating hands) eighths; speak ta and ti-ti first, then body percussion only (think the syllables). Choose two cards; each becomes an ostinato when the teacher points — not both at once. Add Amos Moses (Rhythmically Moving 8) if you have the recording. Homeroom: cards on the board; seated pats and claps.

The Gallant Ship (book pp. 101–102)

The Gallant Ship by title and page only — echo by phrase; prepare orchestration with body percussion (pat = barred, clap = hand drum, shake fists = maracas); perform with teacher bass xylophone if available; transfer to instruments and talk about different timbres. Introduce B as a teacher/student conversation (“She sank?” / “She sank!”). Form: AB repeated; students rotate to the next instrument during each B. Homeroom: one barred instrument plus maracas and a hand drum in a short rotation line.

Notating SML · Jump Jim Joe · 10-second checks (book p. 103)

Notating so–mi–la (review idea from p. 90): echo short fragments (Lucy Locket or Bell Horses), then place disks on three-line staffs. Jump Jim Joe (traditional favourite): partners with hands joined — jump; shake/nod/tap; turn; choose a new partner. Demonstrate with one student; grow the group as partners multiply. Rotating check, group B (about 7 children): 10-second solo — notate or point one so–mi–la fragment. Mark Y or N.

How to use this book

When: Day 2, before The Gallant Ship (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Animals crash the concert. Show a quiet musician, then a wild animal.”
  • Soft pat on a pulse.

During reading

  • Freeze as a statue musician on calm pages; wiggle on wild pages.
  • Whisper “different sounds” once.

After reading

Name one timbre you heard in the story voice (loud, soft, scratchy).

Quick check: “Were all the animal sounds the same?”

Adapt: Seated freeze/wiggle only.

Extend: Pat = barred idea; clap = drum idea once.

Teacher notes

  • GAMEPLAN May Week Three (pp. 101–103). Rhythm cards, Amos Moses game, The Gallant Ship, notating so–mi–la, and Jump Jim Joe by title and page only for arranged songs. Jump Jim Joe is traditional.
  • Homeroom kit: rhythm cards, one barred instrument, maracas, hand drum, staff cards/disks, open carpet for partners.
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group B (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Playing two ostinato cards at once.
  • Copying Gallant Ship notation onto the board.
  • Partner turns that skip choosing a new partner.

Challenge

Lead the B conversation once while the class answers.
