Week 19 · So–Mi, Notes & Reading · Feb 8–12

Rhythm Cards, Line Notes and Kangaroo

We read four-beat rhythm cards with note heads, learn line notes vs space notes with body shapes, review so–mi echoes, and play The Kangaroo with so–mi answers and gentle barred jumps.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

Feb 8–12

Full week: every Grade 1 class gets all 3 classes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Bring: Grade One rhythm cards or board rows (ta, ti-ti, rest), Mole Music if you have it

Day 2 · Practice

Bring: Board with a two-line staff, markers

GAMEPLAN book, Day 2: pp. 56–57

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi.
  3. 5 minLabeling Line and Space Notes (book pp. 56–57) — Two-line staff: a line note sits on a line; a space note sits between lines. Body: fingertips to temples (line note) or one hand on head and one under chin (space note). Children draw line or space notes on the board in short turns.
  4. 6 minMelodic Training review (book p. 57) — Standing circle: echo four-beat so–mi patterns with signs (see p. 50). Optional solfege ladder with only S and M showing.
  5. 5 minLine or space? — You say “line” or “space”; class makes the body shape.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Line-note arms, then walk.

Day 3 · Transfer

Bring: Barred instrument set to C pentatonic if possible (remove F and B), optional kangaroo mask, Bounce High

GAMEPLAN book, Day 3: pp. 57–58

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi.
  3. 5 minThe Kangaroo (book pp. 57–58) — Teach from the book (title and page only). Class finds the repeated word “jump.” So–mi echoes in the third phrase. Game: one kangaroo jumps on each “jump,” points to four children for so–mi food answers; class echoes. Then kangaroo and soloists play any two C-pentatonic notes on each “jump” on the barred instrument. Homeroom: one instrument; short turns; soft jumps in place.
  4. 5 minBounce High review + 10-second checks — On this site — keep the bounce on the pulse. Rotating check, group C (about 7 children): while the game runs, give each child a 10-second solo: speak one four-beat rhythm card, then freeze. Mark Y or N on the record sheet.
  5. 6 minKangaroo + card checks — Checked children speak one rhythm card and sing one so–mi food answer.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Quiet kangaroo jumps to the door.

Story suggestion

Mole Music · David McPhail

Careful mallet playing grows into music — the same care we use on barred instruments for The Kangaroo jumps.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (Story Time with Charles on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Day 3, before The Kangaroo barred turns (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Mole plays with care. Our mallets bounce — they do not pound.”
  • Show Rest, Ready, Play once with pencils.

During reading

  • When Mole practices, mime a gentle bounce on your knees.
  • When others listen, freeze mallets on shoulders.

After reading

Four quiet jumps on the pulse (pat–pat–pat–pat).

Quick check: “What does a kind mallet look like?”

Adapt: Pencil mallets on desks.

Extend: A child models Play–Freeze; the class copies.

Watch-outs

  • Skipping rests on the cards.
  • Pounding the barred instrument.
  • Kangaroo jumps that leave the carpet.

Challenge

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Bounce High, Bounce Low

Traditional singing game · public domain · so–mi–la · 2/4

so = G, la = A, mi = E

Game: Soft ball or beanbag. Underhand only. Swap in a class name for Shiloh.

La is the new neighbour above so. Sign it higher, sing it lightly.

Teddy Bear

Traditional action song · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Turn around, touch the ground, show your shoe, that will do — one action per phrase.

Actions stay small so the tune survives.
