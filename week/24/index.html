This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi–la

Greet on so–mi; add one soft la above so.

Teach

SML echoes · Lucy Locket · All Around the Circle (book p. 71)

Standing circle: echo four-beat so–mi–la patterns with hand signs; point to a solfege ladder that shows only S, M, and L. Lucy Locket (on this site): review melody with signs; hide or find the pocket with kind loud/quiet. All Around the Circle by title and page only — echo by phrase with a chord bordun if you have one; four students sing their names; class echoes. Homeroom: name solos seated; pocket near the carpet edge.

Hunting for Instruments! (book p. 72)

Title and page only. Echo the hunting song; add a pulse ostinato; review how to hold each unpitched instrument. Partners use icon cards: one child plays a hidden instrument; class holds up the matching card. Homeroom: instruments behind a screen or under a cloth; cards on desks.

Reacting to Dynamics · March is Here Now (book p. 73)

Seated fists closed for quiet and open for loud while a short Mozart (or any clear loud/quiet) excerpt plays. List loud and quiet animals; move silently as the chosen animals when the recording repeats. March is Here Now by title and page only — echo text; choose dynamics for lion and lamb parts; body percussion once, then speech again. Homeroom: animal moves beside desks.

How to use this book

When: Day 3, before Reacting to Dynamics (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Some pages feel loud. Some feel quiet. Show me with your hands.”
  • Fist = quiet; open hand = loud.

During reading

  • Open or close hands on each page without talking.
  • Freeze on one favourite quiet page.

After reading

Name one classroom sound that can be loud and quiet.

Quick check: “Was that page loud or quiet?”

Adapt: Two picture cards only: lion / lamb.

Extend: Whisper “lamb” and speak “lion” on the pulse.

Activity · Read-aloud · The Loud Book!

Use the book guide. Loud and quiet hands.

Close

Sign so–la–so–mi once, then walk.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Rain, Rain, Go Away

English nursery rhyme · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Finger rain on the beat. Freeze on the rests.

The classic so–mi reading song. Sign so (higher) and mi (lower) while you sing.

Somebody’s Knocking at Your Door

African American spiritual · traditional, public domain · call and response · 2/4

so = G

Game: Teacher call, class knock-knock on the response. Credit the spiritual tradition.

Knock on the beat, not the words, if you are practising pulse.

So–mi–la piano

G is so, E is mi, A is la. Tap to hear. Grade 1 lives on the first three.
