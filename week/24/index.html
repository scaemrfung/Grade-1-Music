This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Each 20 minutes has the same shape: hello on the pulse (2), voice or body (2), one idea (8), a game (6), close and line up on the pulse (2). Planned for a homeroom: carpet circle or beside desks, pitches from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and a short “Bring” list for each class.

How to use this book

When: Day 2, in the idea slot (about 6 min).

Before reading

  • “Rain, Rain is part of our so–mi family. Which other songs sound like it?” (See-Saw, Star Light.)
  • Silent signs first: so, mi, so, mi.

During reading

  • Sing each page. On every phrase, one partner leads a mirror move; switch on the next phrase.
  • Let a different child be the “phrase leader” for each verse.
  • If a phrase drifts out of tune, echo just that phrase three times, then turn the page.

After reading

Phrase mirrors with Rain, Rain.

Quick check: “How many phrases were in one verse?”

Adapt: Children who aren’t ready to sign can point up and down. A shy singer can sign while the class sings.

Extend: Hum the tune without words. Can the class name the song from the signs alone?

Activity · Partner mirror phrases

Partners face each other; A leads a move for phrase 1, B copies; swap for phrase 2.

Close

Line up.

Teacher notes

  • Call and response = two phrases talking.
  • Mirrors: slow moves only.
  • Invite volunteers for solo calls; never force.
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group D (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Mirror moves that are too fast.
  • Forcing solo calls.

Challenge

Sing a call on your own.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Rain, Rain, Go Away

English nursery rhyme · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Finger rain on the beat. Freeze on the rests.

The classic so–mi reading song. Sign so (higher) and mi (lower) while you sing.

Somebody’s Knocking at Your Door

African American spiritual · traditional, public domain · call and response · 2/4

so = G

Game: Teacher call, class knock-knock on the response. Credit the spiritual tradition.

Knock on the beat, not the words, if you are practising pulse.
