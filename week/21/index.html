Week 21 · Ti-ti, La & Phrases · Feb 22–26

Shapes, Valentine and I Hope!

We move with same/different shapes, play so–so–mi Valentine on a barred instrument, notate so–mi with partners, read I Hope! with a mail-carrier drum game, and hide Lucy Locket’s pocket with kind loud and quiet.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

Feb 22–26

Full week: every Grade 1 class gets all 3 classes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi

Greet on so–mi. One shape freeze.

Teach

Moving with Shapes (book p. 62)

Class assigns a locomotor move to each shape (march, gallop, tip-toe…). Hold up a shape; class moves and freezes when it goes down. Homeroom: small steps beside desks.

Valentine / SSM on bars (book p. 62)

Review Teddy Bear with so–so–mi signs; substitute “Valentine.” Demonstrate S–S–M (G–G–E) on a vertical glockenspiel (only G and E if you can). Half play SSM; half do actions. Homeroom kit: one carried-in glock or xylo; short turns.

Notating So–Mi · I Hope! · Lucy Locket (book pp. 63–64)

Pairs notate SSM/SMS on three-line staff cards (melody moves left to right). I Hope! by title and page only — mail carrier delivers a numbered Valentine card; recipient plays that rhythm on conga or hand drum (alternate hands). Lucy Locket (on this site): hide the pocket; sing quieter when far and louder when near — loud is not a shout. Homeroom: one drum in the circle.

Story suggestion

The Day It Rained Hearts · Felicia Bond

Sharing paper hearts matches the Valentine mail game — take a turn, deliver with care, celebrate kindness.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (StoryTime at Awnie's House on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Day 2, before I Hope! (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Cornelia makes hearts for friends. We will deliver Valentine rhythm cards.”
  • Pat a quiet pulse.

During reading

  • On each heart gift, freeze and smile once.
  • Keep voices soft — kindness is quiet too.

After reading

One kind sentence whispered on the pulse.

Quick check: “What is a kind delivery?”

Adapt: Seated sharing; pass one paper heart.

Extend: A child delivers one imaginary card; class pats the pulse.

Activity · Read-aloud · The Day It Rained Hearts

Use the book guide. Kind deliveries.

Close

Quiet tip-toe to the door on a triangle shape.

  • Pounding the barred instrument.
  • Shouting for loud in Lucy Locket.

Challenge

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Pease Porridge Hot

English nursery rhyme · public domain · rhythm chant (ta and rest) · 2/4

Spoken. Add so–mi later if you wish.

Game: Partner clapping on the tas; open hands on the rest.

The rest after hot and cold is the whole point. Do not skip it.

Lucy Locket

Traditional singing game · public domain · so–mi–la · 2/4

so = G

Game: Circle. One walker drops a beanbag behind a friend on the last phrase. That friend walks next. Keep singing.

If the tune falls apart, sit and sing once, then play again.

Practice studio

Rhythm pad

Tap a box to cycle ta, ti-ti, rest. Play to hear eight beats.

So–mi–la piano

G is so, E is mi, A is la. Tap to hear. Grade 1 lives on the first three.

Name the sound

Play a mystery sound, then tap the name.
