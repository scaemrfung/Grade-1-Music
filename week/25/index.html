This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi–la

Greet on so–mi–la. One rhythm-card speak.

Teach

Rhythm Cards · Notating So–Mi–La (book p. 74)

Speak rhythm cards with ta and ti-ti; touch shoulders on each rest. Notating SML: when so and mi are around lines, la is in the space above so (review p. 63 idea). Echo short fragments, then notate. Challenge: notate the first four pitches of Bounce High (on this site). Homeroom: paper staffs on desks.

Kite movement · A Kite ABA (book pp. 74–75)

Kite imagery for contour and fast/slow (title and page only — tell the story; students pantomime). A Kite by title and page only: speak then sing with bordun if you have one; three students invent wind sounds (chimes, drum scrape, glissando); sit for song and move for wind to feel same/different/same; choose shapes and discover ABA; point to a simple song/wind/song chart. Homeroom: wind sounds with voices or one small instrument.

Which Instrument Do You Hear? · Written Assessment (book p. 76)

Quick review of hidden unpitched sounds on the chart (see also p. 70). Then Written Assessment #3 by title and page only — or a sheet you draw with six icons: you play one hidden instrument; students circle the match. Homeroom: icons under the document camera.

How to use this book

When: Day 2, before A Kite (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Watch how the kite goes up, dips, and comes back.”
  • Trace a hill shape in the air.

During reading

  • On windy pages, flutter fingers high; on calm pages, hands in laps.
  • Whisper “same” when the kite story feels like coming home.

After reading

Show ABA with two matching shapes and one different shape.

Quick check: “Which part was the wind?”

Adapt: Seated arm movements only.

Extend: One child leads a wind sound; class freezes for the song shape.

Watch-outs

  • Putting la below mi.
  • Moving during the A (song) sections.
  • Guessing before the instrument finishes sounding.

Challenge

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Teddy Bear

Traditional action song · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Turn around, touch the ground, show your shoe, that will do — one action per phrase.

Actions stay small so the tune survives.

Pease Porridge Hot

English nursery rhyme · public domain · rhythm chant (ta and rest) · 2/4

Spoken. Add so–mi later if you wish.

Game: Partner clapping on the tas; open hands on the rest.

The rest after hot and cold is the whole point. Do not skip it.
