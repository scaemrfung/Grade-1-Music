Week 05 · Voice & Echo · October

Singing Games

A song can be a game. We sing, we move, we take turns, we keep the tune.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

If you have GAMEPLAN Grade 1. Singing games from the folk list (Lucy Locket, Bounce High). Play from the book; the scores on this site are the public-domain tunes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Class 1 introduces, Class 2 practises, Class 3 applies and checks. Planned for a homeroom: carpet circle or beside desks, pitches from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and a short “Bring” list for each class.

Class 1 · Introduce

Bring: No instruments. Beanbag for the game

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 5 minBounce High, Bounce Low — Seated bounce on the beat, then standing beside desks. Swap in a class name.
  3. 4 minA singing game has a job — We sing the whole time. If the tune falls apart: freeze, sing once sitting, play again.
  4. 7 minRead-aloud · We're Going on a Bear Hunt — Brain break in a circle at the carpet, per the book guide.
  5. 4 minStill a singing voice — Light voice, strong beat in the feet. Sing Bounce High once more that way.

Use in class

  • 🏀Bouncing ballBounce High, Bounce Low: the ball lands on each beat while the class does the seated bounce.

Class 2 · Practise

Bring: No instruments. Beanbag for the game

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 3 minBounce High — Standing, with a class name.
  3. 7 minLucy Locket — Teach it by echo, then circle up at the carpet. One child walks the beat around the outside of the circle with the beanbag and drops it behind a friend.
  4. 3 minKind turns — Everyone gets a turn over the year. No grabbing. No “you missed.”
  5. 7 minPlay the game — Lucy Locket again; sit after two or three turns to reset the tune, then play on.

Use in class

  • 🎤SingalongBounce High: sing along with the bouncing words before adding the class name.

Class 3 · Apply & review

Bring: No instruments. Beanbag for the game

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 3 minBounce High — Seated bounce to settle.
  3. 10 minPlay the game (extended) — Your choice of Lucy Locket or Bounce High, played longer so more children get a turn. Reset the tune sitting between rounds.
  4. 3 minChallenge duet — A pair sings Bounce High while the class pats the beat.
  5. 4 minClose + Check — Sit and sing the song once with no game. Go through the Check list. Stamp if the tune survived the game.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Bounce High, Bounce Low

Seated bounce on the beat, then standing. Words: “Bounce high, bounce low, bounce the ball to Shiloh.” Swap in a class name. So–mi–la sneaks in; do not lecture it yet.

Teach

A singing game has a job

We sing the whole time. The game is not a recess shout. If the tune falls apart, freeze, sing once sitting, then play again.

Lucy Locket

Circle. One child walks the beat on the outside with a “pocket” (beanbag). At the end of the verse they drop it behind a friend. That friend walks next. Keep the singing going.

Kind turns

Everyone gets a turn over the year, not always today. We do not grab. We do not say “you missed.”

Still a singing voice

Excitement makes shouts. Remind: light voice, strong beat in the feet.

Story suggestion

We're Going on a Bear Hunt · Michael Rosen (illus. Helen Oxenbury)

Classic chanted/sung adventure with actions—song played as a class game.

Buy on Amazon · Watch Michael Rosen perform it (Walker Books on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Brain break between Teach and Play, standing in a circle (about 7 min).

Before reading

  • “Listen for the part that comes back again and again. That’s our chorus.”
  • “In a singing game we keep our voices light, even when it gets exciting.”

During reading

  • Chant the chorus (“We’re going on a bear hunt…”) with a pat-clap steady beat. Everyone joins.
  • “We can’t go over it. We can’t go under it. Oh no! We’ve got to go through it!” Hands go over, then under, on the beat.
  • Give a small group a turn for each sound word: swishy swashy grass (rub palms), splash splosh river (pat laps), squelch squerch mud (gentle stamps), stumble trip forest, hoooo wooooo snowstorm (voices slide high and low), tiptoe cave (whisper).
  • Run home fast, then freeze under the covers. Talk about how the tempo sped up.

After reading

Play Lucy Locket or Bounce High with the Bear Hunt rule: light voices, strong beat in the feet. Sit and sing once with no game.

Quick check: “What part came back again and again? What happens to our voices in a singing game?”

Adapt: Hold up picture cards (grass, river, mud, forest, snow, cave) so everyone knows what comes next. Children who need to move can lead the actions.

Extend: Add a new obstacle, invent its sound word, and pick a classroom instrument for it.

Activity · Play the game

Run Lucy Locket or Bounce High for the middle of the block. Sit after two or three turns to reset the tune, then play again.

Close

Sit. Sing the song once with no game. That is how we remember it. Stamp if the tune survived the game.

Materials

  • Circle space
  • One singing game (Lucy Locket or Bounce High)
  • Beanbag or small ball optional

Teacher notes

  • Pick walkers who need a win, not only the confident kids.
  • If the class cannot hold the tune and the game, drop the game and just sing this week. Try the game again in week 6.
  • Beanbags on the floor behind a child, not at their head.

Watch-outs

  • Letting the game become tag. Freeze and reset.
  • Too many rules before the first play. Show once, play.

Challenge

A pair sings Bounce High as a duet while the class pats the beat.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Lucy Locket

Traditional singing game · public domain · so–mi–la · 2/4

so = G

Game: Circle. One walker drops a beanbag behind a friend on the last phrase. That friend walks next. Keep singing.

If the tune falls apart, sit and sing once, then play again.

Bounce High, Bounce Low

Traditional singing game · public domain · so–mi–la · 2/4

so = G, la = A, mi = E

Game: Soft ball or beanbag. Underhand only. Swap in a class name for Shiloh.

La is the new neighbour above so. Sign it higher, sing it lightly.
