This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Class 1 introduces, Class 2 practises, Class 3 applies and checks. Planned for a homeroom: carpet circle or beside desks, pitches from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and a short “Bring” list for each class.

Story suggestion

Because · Mo Willems (illus. Amber Ren)

Chain of musical moments leads a child to the stage—celebrates a year of music learning.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (Music With Miss C on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Close of the year: read it before the portfolio page, or as the final 5 minutes before the week 1 hello song (about 6 min).

Before reading

  • “This book is a chain of ‘becauses.’ Listen for how one small thing leads to music.”
  • “What’s one ‘because’ from our year?”

During reading

  • Each time a page starts with “Because…,” the class taps one soft beat. That makes a steady chain.
  • When the girl sits in row C, seat 14 and hears the orchestra, pause. Everyone closes their eyes for five seconds of listening.
  • When she practises and starts to write music, connect it to our own four-beat patterns and so–mi–la tunes.
  • At the end, when her music is played at the concert hall, ask: “Who might your music change someday?”

After reading

Make a class “because” chain aloud: “Because we learned the beat, we could…” Then sing the week 1 hello song and notice how it feels now.

Quick check: “Name one big music idea from this year: beat, long and short, high and low, or kind listening.”

Adapt: Give sentence starters (“Because I…”) and scribe for children who need it.

Extend: Children write one “because” sentence for their letter to next year’s Grade 1 class.

Activity · Portfolio page

Worksheet plus one last song standing. Optional: they teach a so–mi hello to a stuffed guest or a Grade 1 buddy from another class.

Close

Sing the very first hello song from week 1. Notice how it feels now. Stamp the year.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Hello Everybody

Traditional greeting · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

Sing so as G (or C if that sits better).

Game: Echo by phrase, then greet two children by name on so–mi.

Keep it light. If so is a strain, drop the whole song. The quiet hand starts and stops.

Rain, Rain, Go Away

English nursery rhyme · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Finger rain on the beat. Freeze on the rests.

The classic so–mi reading song. Sign so (higher) and mi (lower) while you sing.

Bounce High, Bounce Low

Traditional singing game · public domain · so–mi–la · 2/4

so = G, la = A, mi = E

Game: Soft ball or beanbag. Underhand only. Swap in a class name for Shiloh.

La is the new neighbour above so. Sign it higher, sing it lightly.
