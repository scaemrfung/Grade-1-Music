This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. From Week 9 on, each 20 minutes has the same shape: hello on the pulse (2), voice or body (2), one idea (8), a game (6), close and line up on the pulse (2). Weeks 1–8 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Planned for a homeroom: carpet circle or beside desks, pitches from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and a short “Bring” list for each class.

How to use this book

When: Day 2, in the idea slot (about 6 min).

Before reading

  • “Listen for fast music and slow music, loud music and quiet music in Troy’s story.”
  • Hold up the four cards: fast, slow, loud, quiet.

During reading

  • On the parade pages, march in place: STRONG, light, STRONG, light (groups of 2).
  • For Troy practising alone at night, sway slowly: a group of 4 (strong on 1).
  • When he plays with a big band, follow the conductor’s hand: big for beat 1.

After reading

March in 2 on the parade pages, sway in 4 at night.

Quick check: “Was the parade in 2 or 4?”

Adapt: Keep only two cards at first (fast/slow). English learners can match arm size and speed without the words.

Extend: Listen to 20 seconds of a New Orleans brass band. Is it fast or slow? Loud or quiet?

Teacher notes

  • Meter = groups of beats; keep it in the body.
  • Strong ≠ loud.
  • Punchinella: the middle child chooses a gentle move.
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group C (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Strong beats that get loud.
  • Punchinella moves that are unsafe.

Challenge

Play beat 1 on the triangle.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Jim Along Josie

American play-party song · public domain · do–mi–so · 2/4

so = G (do = C)

Game: Walk a circle; the leader changes the move (march, tiptoe, hop) each verse.

March in 2: strong on beat 1.

Punchinella

Traditional singing game · public domain · do–mi–so–la · 4/4

so = G (do = C)

Game: One child in the middle shows a move; everyone copies the move.

Sway in 4. Moves stay gentle and safe.
