This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi–la

Greet on so–mi–la. One soft bunny freeze.

Teach

Rhythm Cards with rests (book p. 80)

Read cards from p. 56 idea. Extension: speak a card aloud only if it has a rest somewhere; if there is no rest, internalize the whole card silently. Homeroom: cards under the document camera.

Acka Backa contour game (book pp. 80–81)

Acka Backa is on this site. Affix word cards on the board so they can move. Speak four cards at a time; sing all on so while pointing; then sing the SML melody and slide cards up or down to show contour; add hand signs; pat the beat. Game: seated circle; teacher points on each beat; the child on “you” moves to play the chord bordun; rotate. Homeroom: one instrument; waiting students pat the bordun on laps.

Bunnies on the Move! (book p. 81)

Title and page only — scan ends mid-activity (p. 82 not in the March PDF). Echo the bunny text; keep voices quiet; explore different quiet locomotor or nonlocomotor moves while temple blocks (or two woods) cue change. Same/different idea: freeze in a new shape when the pattern changes. Homeroom: quiet tip-toe beside desks. Teacher note from the book: no new objectives — you may slide this week to the week before Easter and shift later lessons forward one week.

How to use this book

When: Day 3, before Bunnies on the Move! (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Bunnies can be busy and still quiet.”
  • Show a quiet finger on lips, then pat a soft pulse.

During reading

  • On each cake-making page, tip-toe fingers on the floor once.
  • Freeze in a bunny shape on the last page.

After reading

Whisper one way a bunny can move.

Quick check: “Was our voice loud or quiet?”

Adapt: Seated ear-wiggle and nose-twitch only.

Extend: Class tip-toes four beats; freeze on rest.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Jim Along Josie

American play-party song · public domain · do–mi–so · 2/4

so = G (do = C)

Game: Walk a circle; the leader changes the move (march, tiptoe, hop) each verse.

March in 2: strong on beat 1.

Punchinella

Traditional singing game · public domain · do–mi–so–la · 4/4

so = G (do = C)

Game: One child in the middle shows a move; everyone copies the move.

Sway in 4. Moves stay gentle and safe.
