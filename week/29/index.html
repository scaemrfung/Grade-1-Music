Sugar Sugar, Willum and Tres Pajaritos

We read rhythm cards with rests on the shoulders, rotate through Sugar Sugar on barred instruments (wood vs metal), invent Willum verses with leaders, sing Tres Pajaritos with contour and call/response, and review Go All Around the Village.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

Apr 26–30

Full week: every Grade 1 class gets all 3 classes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi–la

Greet on so–mi–la.

Teach

Rhythm cards · Sugar Sugar (book pp. 86–87)

Warm-up: speak each rhythm card; touch shoulders on rests. Sugar Sugar by title and page only — clap/pat the companion rhythms; speak then think the poem; transfer to barred instruments in C pentatonic (hands together on clap measures, alternating on the pat measure). Rotate on a drum cue: speak, then play. Challenge: clap parts on metal, pat parts on wood. Homeroom: one or two barred instruments; waiting line pats on laps.

Willum (book p. 87)

Traditional song by rote. Class invents seven chore verses (chopping wood, taking out trash, jumping…); list on the board; seven leaders pantomime while the class copies and sings. Homeroom: leaders at the front of the carpet.

Tres Pajaritos · Village review (book p. 88)

Tres Pajaritos by title and page only — echo text; discuss meaning; trace contour while singing; call/response by measure; prepare a chord bordun and transfer if you have instruments; try boy/girl or solo/class turns. Optional one-mallet glockenspiel challenge on the bird calls. Review Go All Around the Village with new leaders. Homeroom: contour with a finger in the air; one bordun player.

How to use this book

When: Day 3, before Tres Pajaritos (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Listen for birds in the story. Show a quiet beak with your finger.”
  • Soft so–mi on “tweet.”

During reading

  • Trace a small contour in the air on bird pages.
  • Freeze like a listening bird on one page.

After reading

Echo one “pip-a-ri-a” bird call quietly.

Quick check: “Did the bird sounds go up or down?”

Adapt: Seated listening only.

Extend: Call/response: teacher tweets, class echoes.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

See-Saw

Traditional · public domain (also sung as Saw, Saw, Saw Away) · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Partners hold hands and bend on so, stretch on mi — or the reverse, as long as the class agrees.

A small knee bend on each so keeps the beat honest.

Bounce High, Bounce Low

Traditional singing game · public domain · so–mi–la · 2/4

so = G, la = A, mi = E

Game: Soft ball or beanbag. Underhand only. Swap in a class name for Shiloh.

La is the new neighbour above so. Sign it higher, sing it lightly.
