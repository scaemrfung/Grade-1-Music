Week 17 · So–Mi, Notes & Reading · Jan 18–22

So–Mi Patterns and Shape Moves

We echo phrase-length body patterns and so–mi hand-sign patterns, build ta/ti-ti on the board, move with same/different shapes, play so–so–mi on a barred instrument for Valentine, and notate so–mi on a three-line staff.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

Jan 18–22

Full week: every Grade 1 class gets all 3 classes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Day 1 · Prepare

Bring: Hand drum, hand-signal cards if posted, board and marker

GAMEPLAN book, Day 1: p. 50

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi. Short phrase-echo clap (book p. 38 idea).
  3. 5 minRhythmic Training (book p. 50) — Review four-measure phrase echoes in 2/4 with one, two, or three body-percussion levels (stamp with alternating feet). Homeroom: stay beside desks.
  4. 6 minMelodic Training — SM (book p. 50) — Standing circle: echo four-beat so–mi patterns with hand signs (start on so). Light singing tone; good posture.
  5. 5 minTa to ti-ti on the board (book p. 50) — Draw four tas; speak, speak-and-clap, clap. Change one ta to ti-ti and repeat. Prepares the paper assessment later.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — So–mi tip-toe to the door.

Use in class

  • 👏Body percussionRhythmic Training: phrase-length body levels.
  • 🦜So–mi–la echoMelodic Training: so–mi with signs.

Music time: 20 min · plus 5 min devotional

Day 2 · Practice

Bring: Form-shape cards or board sketches (square, circle, triangle…), one glockenspiel or xylophone with only G and E if possible, Up, Up, Down if you have it

GAMEPLAN book, Day 2: pp. 51–52

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi.
  3. 5 minMoving with Shapes (book p. 51) — Four or five children pick shapes. Class assigns a locomotor move to each (march, gallop, tip-toe). Hold up a shape → move; put it down → freeze. Extension: a student conductor points along a shape row. Homeroom: small self-space beside desks; skip gallop if space is tight.
  4. 6 minValentine / SSM on bars (book pp. 51–52) — Review Teddy Bear with so–so–mi signs. Substitute “Valentine” for every so–so–mi. Demonstrate S–S–M (G–G–E) on a vertical glockenspiel (only G and E if you can). One child plays; then half play SSM on the barred instrument while half do Teddy actions. Homeroom kit: one carried-in glock or xylo; rotate short turns.
  5. 5 minRead-aloud · Up, Up, Down — Use the book guide. High and low bodies.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Valentine so–so–mi to the door.

Use in class

  • 🧊Freeze danceMoving with Shapes: move and freeze.
  • 🐱Copycat songsValentine: so–so–mi on bars.

Music time: 20 min · plus 5 min devotional

Day 3 · Transfer

Bring: Three-line staff cards and disks if you have them (or paper staffs), Engine Engine

GAMEPLAN book, Day 3: p. 52

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi.
  3. 5 minNotating So-Mi (book p. 52) — Pairs share one three-line staff. Middle line = so; partners place mi. You sing fragments (SSM, SMS); class echoes, then notates. Melody moves left to right — disks do not stack. Extension: notate a bit of Valentine, Engine Engine, or Lucky Me.
  4. 5 minWhat Shall We Do When We All Go Out? (book p. 51) + 10-second checks — Teach from the book (title and page only). Echo by phrase. After the song, one outdoor or winter idea; class pantomimes while you keep a pulse on drum or piano. Homeroom: scattered beside desks. Rotating check, group A (about 7 children): while the game runs, give each child a 10-second solo: place so and mi on a three-line staff, then freeze. Mark Y or N on the record sheet.
  5. 6 minStaff + shape checks — Checked children place so and mi on a staff and show one shape move on cue.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — March four tas to the door.

Use in class

  • 🪜Solfège staircaseNotating So-Mi: three-line staff.

Story suggestion

How to use this book

When: Day 2, after Valentine / SSM on bars (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Up is high like so; down is low like mi.”
  • Practise so–so–mi once with signs.

During reading

  • Reach up on high words; crouch on low words.
  • Keep a quiet ta in the feet.

After reading

Four so–so–mi patterns with signs.

Quick check: “Did our bodies match high and low?”

Adapt: Finger ups and downs beside desks.

Extend: A child points high or low; the class sings so or mi.

Activity · Ta to ti-ti on the board (book p. 50)

Draw four tas; speak, speak-and-clap, clap. Change one ta to ti-ti and repeat. Prepares the paper assessment later.

Close

So–mi tip-toe to the door.

Watch-outs

  • Galloping in a crowded aisle.
  • Stacking staff disks under each other.
  • Rushing the ta-to-ti-ti board game.

Challenge

Conduct the shape row once.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Teddy Bear

Traditional action song · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Turn around, touch the ground, show your shoe, that will do — one action per phrase.

Actions stay small so the tune survives.

Engine, Engine Number Nine

Traditional playground chant · public domain · so–mi (or spoken on the beat) · 2/4

so = G

Game: Walk the beat like a train. Freeze on the rest.

Teach it spoken on the pulse first, then sung so–mi. Chicago can become a local town.

Practice studio

So–mi–la piano

G is so, E is mi, A is la. Tap to hear. Grade 1 lives on the first three.
