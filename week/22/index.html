This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–19 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Day 1 · Prepare

Bring: Sousa march or strong-beat recording, Drummer Hoff book if you have it, hand drums with mallets

GAMEPLAN book, Day 1: p. 65

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi. One move-and-freeze.
  3. 5 minMove and Freeze (book p. 65) — Move when the march plays; freeze when it stops — levels and directions. Homeroom: march in place.
  4. 6 minDrummer Hoff ostinato (book pp. 65–66) — Speak Drum-mer Hoff (rest); transfer to hand drums. Quiet tremolo on Ready–Aim–Fire; one accent on Kahbahblooom. Homeroom: rotate a few drums.
  5. 5 minRead-aloud · Drummer Hoff — Use the book guide. Ostinato and accent.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Three pats and a rest to the door.

Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi

Greet on so–mi. One move-and-freeze.

Teach

Move and Freeze · Drummer Hoff (book pp. 65–66)

Scattered move when the march plays; freeze when it stops — explore levels and directions. Drummer Hoff by title and page only: speak the ostinato, transfer to hand drums with mallets, quiet tremolo on “Ready… Aim… Fire!” and one accent on “Kahbahblooom!” Dramatize with a few actors. Homeroom: drums in a small rotation; soft mallets.

Celebrate the Presidents! (book p. 66)

Title and page only. March while you echo the poem; build a four-beat ostinato from Lincoln (ta ta) and Washington (ti-ti ti-ti) cards; speak it four times with cheer actions; return to the poem. Homeroom: march in place beside desks.

Patriotic Sing · She’ll Be Comin’ ’Round the Mountain (book p. 67)

Book lists Yankee Doodle and America by title and page — use O Canada (on this site) for our repertoire; stand respectfully if you play a recording. She’ll Be Comin’ by title and page only — add verse actions; reverse actions at the end of the fourth phrase. Homeroom: actions in place.

Story suggestion

Drummer Hoff · adapted by Barbara Emberley

The story’s cannon crew matches the ostinato and drum accents in this week’s GAMEPLAN lesson.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (Storybooks Read Along on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Day 1, with Drummer Hoff (about 5–8 min).

Before reading

  • “Listen for who helps load the cannon.”
  • Pat three quiet beats and a rest: Drum-mer Hoff (sh).

During reading

  • Whisper the ostinato while you read.
  • On Kahbahblooom, one accent clap — then freeze.

After reading

Speak the ostinato once more with shoulders on the rest.

Quick check: “When do the drums stay quiet?”

Adapt: Pat laps instead of drums.

Extend: Three children play the ostinato; the class whispers.

Activity · Read-aloud · Drummer Hoff

Use the book guide. Ostinato and accent.

Close

March four beats in place, then walk.

Teacher notes

  • GAMEPLAN February Week Three (pp. 65–67). Drummer Hoff, Celebrate the Presidents!, patriotic titles, and She’ll Be Comin’ by title and page only — no book melody copied here. O Canada (first phrase) is on this site.
  • Canadian classroom: prefer O Canada for the patriotic stand; the book’s US titles stay as optional references.
  • Homeroom kit: a few hand drums with mallets, one march recording, president word cards.
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group B (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Loud Kahbahblooom that startles the class.
  • Running during Move and Freeze.
  • Copying book lyrics onto the board.

Challenge
