Week 11 · Ta, Tempo & Contour · Nov 16–20

Fast and Slow

The pulse can go fast or slow and still stay steady. We walk and freeze in Walk and Stop, wait through a whole musical sentence, pass a woodblock on the pulse, and share what we are thankful for.

3 classes a week × 30 min · Each class: devotional song 5 min + music 20 min

Nov 16–20

Full week: every Grade 1 class gets all 3 classes.

This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Weeks 1–12 follow the GAMEPLAN lesson order, so their blocks vary in length but always add up to 20. Later months use the same approach as each month is rewritten. Weeks 5 on are planned for a homeroom (carpet circle or beside desks, kit carried in). Weeks 1–4 are written for the Music room (floor spots, instruments set out, room to move) and carry a short homeroom note. Pitches come from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and each class has a short “Bring” list.

Day 1 · Prepare

Bring: Hand drum

GAMEPLAN book, Day 1: p. 32

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi. Pat slow ta, then a little faster.
  3. 6 minWalk and Stop (book p. 32) — Teach the song from the book (also scored on this site). Walk on the pulse while singing; freeze on “stop.” Change how long you wait. Freeze in a high or low statue that matches the end of the phrase.
  4. 5 minWait a Phrase (book p. 32) — Drum a clear four-beat sentence. Children walk for one sentence and wait through the next (tap the drum rim on waiting sentences). Homeroom: walk the perimeter of the carpet; Music room next year: scatter through the open floor.
  5. 5 minTempo choose — A child picks slow or fast for the next Walk and Stop. Class matches.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Slow walk; freeze at the door.

Bring: Woodblocks (2–3) and beaters, hand drum, We’re Going on a Bear Hunt if you have it

  3. 5 minI Can Play the Wood Block (book p. 33) — Teach the song from the book (title and page only). Pass two or three woodblocks around the circle on the pulse so every child gets a short turn. Teacher keeps a quiet accompaniment with voice or drum.

  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Pass the last woodblock home, then walk.

Day 3 · Transfer

Bring: Hand drum, turkey high/low pictures or board drawings

GAMEPLAN book, Day 3: p. 34

  1. 5 minDevotional song — Your opening song. Not part of the music plan below.
  2. 2 minHello Song, so–mi — Greet on so–mi.
  3. 5 minGobble Gobble Gobble (book p. 34) — Teach the song from the book (title and page only). Show high and low turkey pictures; sing high or low (so–mi feel) as you point. Add the circle game: one child walks the pulse around the outside and taps a head on the last word.
  4. 5 minHigh/low statues — Freeze high or low when the drum stops.
  5. 6 minWalk and Stop review + 10-second checks — One more round. Checked children lead a freeze statue. Rotating check, group C (about 7 children): while the game runs, give each child a 10-second solo: walk four slow tas, then four faster tas, and freeze on the stop. Mark Y or N on the record sheet.
  6. 2 minLine up on the pulse — Gobble quietly on ta to the door.

Story suggestion

We're Going on a Bear Hunt · Michael Rosen (illus. Helen Oxenbury)

The story speeds up for the run home and slows for careful walking — a clear picture of fast and slow pulse.

Buy on Amazon · Watch Michael Rosen perform it (Walker Books on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Day 2, after Walk and Stop (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Listen for when the story goes fast and when it goes slow.”
  • Practise once: walk in place slowly, then tiptoe faster, still even.

During reading

  • Chant the chorus on a slow pat.
  • At each new place, keep the pulse but change the move.
  • On the run home, speed up the pats — still even, not a scramble. Then freeze.

After reading

Walk slow to the door, then four fast tiptoes in place.

Quick check: “When did the story go fast? Slow?”

Adapt: Tiptoe in place beside desks if the carpet is small.

Extend: A child conductor shows slow or fast with their hand; the class matches.

Activity · Tempo choose

A child picks slow or fast for the next Walk and Stop. Class matches.

Close

Slow walk; freeze at the door.

Teacher notes

  • Tempo is speed — do not mix it with loud/quiet.
  • Woodblocks: two or three in the circle is enough for a homeroom kit.
  • Gobble Gobble prepares so–mi; keep the words high/low for now.
  • Phrase is felt by walking and waiting; we name “phrase” later (Week 24).
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group C (Day 3).
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group C (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Fast becoming a run.
  • Passing the woodblock off the pulse.
  • Shouting on high notes.

Challenge

Lead Walk and Stop and choose the waiting time.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Walk and Stop

Traditional movement game · public domain · chant on the beat · 4/4

Teacher drum is the boss.

Game: Walk the pulse. Statue on stop. Later: hop, then stop; tiptoe, then stop.

Own space. Soft knees. This is pulse, not racing.

Hello Everybody

Traditional greeting · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

Sing so as G (or C if that sits better).

Game: Echo by phrase, then greet two children by name on so–mi.

Keep it light. If so is a strain, drop the whole song. The quiet hand starts and stops.
