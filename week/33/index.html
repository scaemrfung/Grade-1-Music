This week in 3 classes

Each class is about 30 minutes: your devotional song (5 min), then 20 minutes of music from this page, with a few minutes for transitions. Day 1 prepares, Day 2 practises, Day 3 transfers and checks. Each 20 minutes has the same shape: hello on the pulse (2), voice or body (2), one idea (8), a game (6), close and line up on the pulse (2). Planned for a homeroom: carpet circle or beside desks, pitches from your voice or the Music Studio on the screen, and a short “Bring” list for each class.

Story suggestion

Mole Music · David McPhail

Mole practices a small violin melody that grows—tiny tune becomes meaningful music.

Buy on Amazon · Watch read-aloud (Story Time with Charles on YouTube)

How to use this book

When: Day 2, in the idea slot (about 6 min).

Before reading

  • “Mole’s first try is not beautiful. Listen for how he gets better.”
  • “Today you’ll make a tiny tune, like Mole’s first song.”

During reading

  • When Mole first draws the bow and it screeches, the class makes one tiny squeak sound (then rests).
  • “He could play one note, then two, then a scale”: sing so, then so–mi, then mi–so–la with signs.
  • When he learns a simple song, you sing a four-beat so–mi–la tune. Class echoes. This is “Mole’s song.”
  • Mole hums his music while he digs. Everyone hums the tune softly.

After reading

Collect three “Mole pieces” and play them for the class.

Quick check: “What made your piece different from your partner’s?”

Adapt: Offer a fill-in frame (so __ mi __) so beginners only pick two notes. Humming counts.

Extend: Collect three “Mole songs” and play them on the site piano for the class.

Scores

Public-domain folk songs on a three-line so–mi–la staff. Play the line, then sing. These are our notations, not GAMEPLAN arrangements.

Rain, Rain, Go Away

English nursery rhyme · public domain · so–mi · 2/4

so = G

Game: Finger rain on the beat. Freeze on the rests.

The classic so–mi reading song. Sign so (higher) and mi (lower) while you sing.

Bounce High, Bounce Low

Traditional singing game · public domain · so–mi–la · 2/4

so = G, la = A, mi = E

Game: Soft ball or beanbag. Underhand only. Swap in a class name for Shiloh.

La is the new neighbour above so. Sign it higher, sing it lightly.
