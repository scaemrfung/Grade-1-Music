Your jobs today

Warm-up · Hello Song, so–mi–la

Greet on so–mi–la.

Teach

Mexican Counting ostinato · Popsicle Stick Game (book p. 98)

Review Mexican Counting Song (p. 96). Class creates an ostinato on a body-percussion staff — start with two levels, then three; four levels are sophisticated for Grade 1. Popsicle Stick Game (review idea from p. 36): notate rhythms from recent songs by feel and title (Lucy Locket, Bounce High, One Two Three, Rain on the Green Grass, Sugar Sugar) — speak ti-ti and ta patterns; do not copy book notation onto the board. Homeroom: sticks on desks; ostinato levels shown with four board circles.

Bell Horses (book pp. 98–99)

Bell Horses by title and page only — discover horses on the middle line as so; sing contour with solfege and hand signs; echo text with melody while pointing to horses; partners one behind the other, hands joined. A: bounce knees in place while singing; B: internalize the song while moving through space. Orchestration from movement: bounce = barred / sleigh bells; clap on “Bell, One, two”; optional coconut shells or wood blocks on the B pulse. Homeroom: one bordun player; partners in an aisle.

Punchinella · share + 10-second checks (book p. 100)

Punchinella (traditional): circle with one student in the centre. Four verses — monkey eyes; pulse-point while Punchinella improvises; class copies; march and choose a new Punchinella. Rotating check, group A (about 7 children): 10-second solo — speak or clap one popsicle-stick rhythm from Day 1. Mark Y or N.

How to use this book

When: Day 2, before Bell Horses (about 5 min).

Before reading

  • “Mole practises alone. Show a tiny tunnel with your hands, then open to a stage.”
  • Soft so on “practise.”

During reading

  • Tiny finger violin on practise pages; freeze when music reaches the surface.
  • Whisper “keep going” once together.

After reading

Echo one so–mi–la horse contour quietly.

Quick check: “Did Mole quit or keep practising?”

Adapt: Finger violin only.

Extend: One child leads a bounce pulse; class joins.

Teacher notes

  • GAMEPLAN May Week Two (pp. 98–100). Mexican Counting Song ostinato, Popsicle Stick Game, Bell Horses, and Punchinella by title and page only for arranged songs. Punchinella is traditional.
  • Homeroom kit: body-percussion counters, popsicle sticks, one barred instrument, sleigh bells if you have them, open carpet circle.
  • Rotating 10-second checks this week: group A (Day 3).

Watch-outs

  • Four body levels at once before the class is ready.
  • Copying Bell Horses notation from the book onto the board.
  • Punchinella turns that run past one verse each.

Challenge

Lead one two-level ostinato under the counting song.
