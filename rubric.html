Grade 1 Music rubric

Reporting outcome (every term): Students understand musical concepts and demonstrate musical skills.

Scale: Not Yet · Meeting · Mastering (NY · M · Ma). Mastering means grade-level insight and transfer, not extra work. Score the music outcome, not behaviour.

Overall rubric

Grade 1 music rubric: four criteria at three levels
CriterionNot YetMeetingMastering
Beat and rhythmI can't keep the beat yet, even when we do it together.I keep a steady beat and clap or play the rhythms we learned.I keep the beat on my own and can make up a short rhythm.
SingingI'm still learning the words and tune.I sing the song with the right words and mostly the right notes.I sing in tune with expression (loud/soft, fast/slow) and can lead a line.
Playing instrumentsI can't play the pattern yet, even with help.I play the pattern we practised, on the beat.I play the pattern with control and can change it a little on purpose.
Listening and movingI don't show what I hear yet.I show high/low, loud/soft, or fast/slow with my body or words.I explain what I hear and connect it to how the music feels.

Assessment rubrics (2 terms)

Scale: Not Yet · Meeting · Mastering. Mastering = grade-level skill used with control and explained, not extra work. Score the music skill, not behaviour.

How each assessment is gathered:

  • O = Observation (I watch and listen as students perform) ·
  • C = Conversation (students explain or answer questions) ·
  • P = Product (students create or hand in work)

Term 1

Grade 1 music assessment rubrics, Term 1
AssessmentNot YetMeetingMastering
O - Music - Steady BeatI can't keep the beat yet, even when we do it together.I pat, step, or tap a steady beat with the song.I keep the beat on my own, even when the song speeds up or slows down.
O - Music - Echo RhythmsI can't clap the rhythm back yet.I clap back ta and ti-ti rhythms the right way.I clap back new rhythms right away and can make one for the class to echo.
O - Music - Singing a Class SongI'm still learning the words and the tune.I sing the song with the right words and mostly the right notes.I sing in tune, clearly, and can sing a part on my own.
O - Music - High/Low PitchI can't tell high from low yet.I show high and low sounds and sing so-mi patterns.I sing so-mi patterns in tune and can show or make up my own.
C - Music - Loud/Soft & Fast/SlowI don't show what I hear yet.I show loud/soft and fast/slow with my body and tell what I hear.I explain what changed in the music and why it sounds that way.

Term 2

Grade 1 music assessment rubrics, Term 2
AssessmentNot YetMeetingMastering
O - Music - Reading Rhythm CardsI can't read the rhythm cards yet.I clap four-beat cards with ta, ti-ti, and rest correctly.I read new cards on my own and can help a friend read one.
O - Music - Instrument PatternI can't play the pattern yet, even with help.I play the pattern on my instrument, on the beat.I play the pattern with control and can change it a little on purpose.
O - Music - Singing with ExpressionI can't change how I sing yet.I sing loud/soft or fast/slow when the music asks.I choose how to sing a part and can tell why it fits the song.
C - Music - Listening ResponseI can't tell about the music yet.I tell if the music is fast/slow, loud/soft, or high/low, and how it makes me feel.I explain what I hear and connect it to how the music feels, using music words.
P - Music - Create & PerformI can't make or play my own pattern yet.I make a four-beat rhythm or so-mi pattern and perform it.I make a pattern on purpose, perform it with control, and explain my choices.

Comment stems

  • Not Yet: “[Name] is still building [skill] and is not yet doing it successfully in class. Next step: …”
  • Meeting: “[Name] uses [skill] correctly in class music-making and understands the idea behind it.”
  • Mastering: “[Name] applies [skill] with intention and can explain or show it to others.”
