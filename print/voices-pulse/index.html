Voices & Pulse

Four voices, the quiet hand, pulse in the body and as a group, and high/low.

Week 1 · Four Voices

Welcome to music class. We meet our four voices (whisper, talk, call, sing), learn the quiet hand, and start every class on a pulse.

Warm-up. Talk, raise your hand, freeze. Practise three times and smile at each freeze. This is our stop signal all year.

Meet four voices. Say “good morning” in a whisper, a talking voice, a calling (playground) voice, then sing it. The class copies each one. Hold up the matching card.

Read-aloud · Mortimer. Use the book guide. The class sings Mortimer’s noise line (singing voice) and speaks the family’s “be quiet” lines (talking voice).

Which voice is best for songs?. Sing one line in each voice. Vote: the light singing voice. We keep calling voices for the playground.

Activity. Point to a card; the class says “good morning” in that voice. Switch fast. The quiet hand stops the game.

Close. Drum a slow pulse. One table at a time walks to the door on the drum, then freezes.

This week in 3 classes (devotional song 5 min + music 20 min each).

Week 2 · Pulse in the Body

Music has a steady pulse, like a heartbeat. We feel it in our bodies, pat it, walk it, and freeze when it stops.

Warm-up. Whisper, talk, sing “ready.” Keep it short.

Find the pulse. Hands on chests: feel your heartbeat. Now pat laps to the drum. Put up 4 heart cards and touch one on each pat. Music has a pulse like a heart. (No “ta” yet.)

Read-aloud · Hand, Hand, Fingers, Thumb. Use the book guide. Everyone pats the pulse the whole time; the words change, the pats stay even.

Walk Engine, Engine. Walk the pulse while chanting. Freeze on the last word, “line.” Try it three times.

Activity. Pat thighs to the drum. When the drum stops, freeze. Change speed a little, never too fast.

Close. Walk to the door on the drum and freeze.

Week 3 · Pulse as a Group

We keep one pulse together as a class: in a circle, passing, walking and playing, all on the same beat.

Warm-up. Whisper, talk, sing “together.” Class echoes.

One class, one pulse. Pat together. Then one table pats while others listen: does it match? Then all together again.

Read-aloud · Giraffes Can’t Dance. Use the book guide. Move in place on the pulse for each animal dance; freeze at page turns.

Play the pulse together. Tap sticks (or pencils, gently) together on the pulse. One group starts, the next joins, then all.

Activity. Pass a beanbag around the circle, one pass per drum beat, while chanting Engine, Engine. Freeze on “line.”

Close. Walk as one train to the door.

Week 4 · High and Low

Sounds can be high or low. We show high and low with our bodies and voices, on drums, woodblock and the See-Saw song.

Warm-up. Slide your voice up like a rocket and down like a slide. Hands follow.

Crouch and stretch. Play the low drum: everyone crouches. Play the high woodblock: everyone stretches tall. Mix them up.

See-Saw partners. Partners hold hands and seesaw up and down with the song. Up on high notes, down on low.

Read-aloud · Rain, Rain, Go Away!. Use the book guide. Hands show high and low on each “rain, rain.”

Activity. Walk on the pulse; when the sound changes, show high or low and freeze.

Close. High tables tiptoe, low tables walk low to the door.
