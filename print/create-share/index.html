Student four-beat pieces, an in-class concertette, picture checks, requests and listening.

Week 33 · My Four-Beat Piece

Each student makes a short four-beat piece with ta, ti-ti and rest, and plays it.

Warm-up. Echo rows.

Make a piece. Each child builds a four-beat piece with cards.

Read-aloud · Mole Music. Use the book guide.

Play your piece. More volunteers share.

Activity. Volunteers play their piece; the class echoes.

Close. Line up.

Week 34 · Our Concertette

We share a four-minute concertette in class and practise being a good audience. O Canada closes.

Warm-up. Slides and so–mi–la echoes.

Plan the concertette. Choose 3 items: a song, a pulse or ostinato piece, and two student pieces. Practise.

Read-aloud · Never Play Music Right Next to the Zoo. Use the book guide.

The concertette. Perform the 4-minute program for a small audience (buddy class or principal).

Activity. Half performs, half is the audience; swap.

Close. Line up.

Week 35 · Picture Check Stations

We show what we know at five picture-check stations: pulse, rest, so–mi, mallet bounce and AB.

Warm-up. Voices and body levels.

Station preview. Walk through the 5 stations: pulse, rest, so–mi, mallet bounce, AB.

Picture check. Children show each station; mark Y/N on the record sheet.

Read-aloud · Because. Use the book guide.

Activity. Groups rotate through practice.

Close. Line up.

Week 36 · Requests and Listening

We choose favourite songs and games, and listen with respect to music of First Nations, Métis and Inuit peoples.

Warm-up. Four voices.

Requests. Children vote for favourite songs and games from the year.

Read-aloud · We Belong to the Drum. Use the book guide.

Student choice. Children lead games.

Activity. Play a favourite game (Bee Bee, Bow Wow Wow, Punchinella).

Close. Line up.
