Introduction, Phrase & Loud/Quiet

Song doorways (introductions), a seasonal pulse, phrases felt, and piano/forte.

Week 13 · The Introduction (Song Doorway)

Many songs start with an introduction: a little doorway before the singing starts. We listen, wait, and come in together.

Warm-up. Tall, hands still, listening.

The doorway. Play 4 triangle taps; the class waits, then sings See-Saw. The taps were the introduction.

Read-aloud · Zin! Zin! Zin! A Violin. Use the book guide. The orchestra waits before playing.

Choose the doorway. Children choose triangle, woodblock or drum for the introduction.

Activity. Learn it by echo. Walk in a spiral line on the pulse.

Close. Triangle doorway, then walk.

This week in 3 classes (devotional song 5 min + music 20 min each).

Week 14 · Seasonal Pulse

We keep the pulse in a seasonal song: the Jingle Bells chorus, with jingles and steps.

Warm-up. “Jin-gle bells” high and light.

Jingle Bells chorus. Learn the first phrase by echo. Pat the pulse.

Read-aloud · Clap Your Hands. Use the book guide. Each move four times.

Add colours. Triangle on the first pulse only, shakers on every pulse.

Activity. Half the class jingles the pulse, half sings. Swap.

Close. Jingle to the door.

Week 15 · Feel the Phrase

Songs are made of phrases, like sentences. We feel each phrase with a rainbow arm and a paper plate.

Warm-up. Breathe in before each phrase.

Phrase rainbows. Sing Rain, Rain; arms draw a rainbow for each phrase. How many?

Read-aloud · Polar Bear, Polar Bear. Use the book guide. Each call and answer is a phrase.

Phrase partners. Partner A sings phrase 1, partner B sings phrase 2.

Activity. Slide a paper plate across the floor for one phrase. Stop when the phrase ends.

Close. Line up.

Week 16 · Quiet and Loud

Music can be quiet (piano) or loud (forte). We choose mouse or lion and control our sound.

Warm-up. Say “hello” like a mouse, then a lion.

Piano and forte. Piano = quiet (mouse). Forte = loud (lion). Show the pictures and play the drum both ways.

Read-aloud · The Loud Book!. Use the book guide.

Play loud and quiet. Shakers play piano and forte as the pictures change.

Activity. The teacher holds up a picture; the class chants Engine, Engine to match.

Close. Mouse feet to the door.
