Week 29 · Pitched Wood and Metal

Some instruments play different pitches. Xylophones are wood; glockenspiels are metal. We hear the difference and match it to woodblock and triangle.

Warm-up. Say “wood” and “metal” in the voice of each sound.

Pitched wood/metal. Play the xylophone (wood bars) and the Studio glockenspiel (metal). Match: xylophone = woodblock family; glockenspiel = triangle family.

Read-aloud · Zin! Zin! Zin! A Violin. Use the book guide.

Play pitches. Bounce High on the xylophone (G–E–A).

Activity. Wood or metal? Point to the mat.

Close. Line up.

Week 30 · The Repeat Sign

Two dots and two lines mean “do it again.” We find repeat signs and play a part twice.

Warm-up. Echo a row twice.

The repeat sign. Show two dots and double lines. “Go back and do it again.” Clap a row with the sign; clap it twice.

Read-aloud · Pete the Cat. Use the book guide.

Play with repeats. Groups play a row with a repeat on woodblock.

Activity. Play a row with or without the sign; the class claps once or twice.

Close. Line up.

Week 31 · ABA: Come Back to A

A piece can go A, then B, then back to A. We compose our own B with ta and rest.

Warm-up. Sway A, march B.

ABA. A: See-Saw. B: a ta/rest row. A: See-Saw again. Show cards A B A.

Read-aloud · From Head to Toe. Use the book guide.

Perform ABA. Pairs play their B between two A’s.

Activity. Move to show A, B, A.

Close. Line up.

Week 32 · The 6/8 Swing

Some songs swing in two big beats, each with three little ones. We feel 6/8 in Hickory Dickory Dock and Jack and Jill.

Warm-up. Swing arms left-right on two big beats.

Hickory swing. Say Hickory Dickory Dock and swing arms. Feel two big beats, each with three little ticks.

Read-aloud · Giraffes Can’t Dance. Use the book guide.

Swing band. Drum on big beats, shakers on little ones.

Activity. Walk the big beats; shakers tick the little ones.

Close. Line up.
