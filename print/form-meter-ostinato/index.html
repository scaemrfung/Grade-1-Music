Snap, clap, pat, stamp; AB form; marching in 2 and swaying in 4; speech ostinato.

Week 25 · Four Levels of Body Percussion

Our bodies have four sound levels: snap, clap, pat and stamp. We play patterns that move between them.

Warm-up. Snap (or finger taps), clap, pat, stamp softly, 4 each.

Four levels. Show the cards from top to bottom: snap (head), clap (chest), pat (knees), stamp (feet).

Read-aloud · Noisy Nora. Use the book guide.

Build a pattern. Groups build a four-beat body pattern and perform it.

Activity. The teacher plays four beats on one level; the class echoes. Mix levels.

Close. Line up.

Week 26 · AB: Two Different Parts

Same/different gets a name: A and B. A song or piece can have two different parts.

Warm-up. Same or different patterns.

Meet A and B. Part A: See-Saw sung. Part B: Engine, Engine chanted. Show A and B cards.

Read-aloud · We’re Going on a Bear Hunt. Use the book guide.

Perform AB. Groups perform their AB.

Activity. A = sway. B = march. Switch when the part changes.

Close. Line up.

Week 27 · Marching in 2, Swaying in 4

Beats come in groups. We march in 2 and sway in 4 with Jim Along Josie and Punchinella.

Warm-up. Pat STRONG-light on knees.

Groups of 2. March: step strong on 1, light on 2. Sing Jim Along Josie.

Read-aloud · Trombone Shorty. Use the book guide.

March or sway?. The teacher plays 2 or 4; the class marches or sways. Triangle on beat 1.

Activity. Walk in a circle; the leader changes the move (march, tiptoe, hop) each verse.

Close. March in 2.

Week 28 · Ostinato: A Pattern That Repeats

An ostinato is a short pattern that repeats under a song. We start with speech ostinatos.

Warm-up. “Rain-y day” (ti-ti ta), echo.

Speech ostinato. Say “rain-y day” over and over while the teacher sings Rain, Rain.

Read-aloud · Max Found Two Sticks. Use the book guide.

Layer it. Groups perform their ostinato under Acka Backa.

Activity. Half says the ostinato, half sings. Swap.

Close. Line up saying the ostinato.
