Unit 7 · Print pack

SML, Dynamics, ABA & Timbre

So–mi–la and dynamics with hunting instruments, notate la and label ABA with A Kite, Jack and Jill contour with wood/metal/skin families, then Acka Backa and quiet bunnies.

Week 24 · SML, Hunting Instruments and March is Here Now

We echo so–mi–la with hand signs and the solfege ladder, review Lucy Locket loud and quiet, sing All Around the Circle with name solos, hunt and name unpitched instruments, react to loud and quiet with Mozart, and speak March is Here Now with lion and lamb dynamics.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi; add one soft la above so.

SML echoes · Lucy Locket · All Around the Circle (book p. 71). Standing circle: echo four-beat so–mi–la patterns with hand signs; point to a solfege ladder that shows only S, M, and L. Lucy Locket (on this site): review melody with signs; hide or find the pocket with kind loud/quiet. All Around the Circle by title and page only — echo by phrase with a chord bordun if you have one; four students sing their names; class echoes. Homeroom: name solos seated; pocket near the carpet edge.

Hunting for Instruments! (book p. 72). Title and page only. Echo the hunting song; add a pulse ostinato; review how to hold each unpitched instrument. Partners use icon cards: one child plays a hidden instrument; class holds up the matching card. Homeroom: instruments behind a screen or under a cloth; cards on desks.

Reacting to Dynamics · March is Here Now (book p. 73). Seated fists closed for quiet and open for loud while a short Mozart (or any clear loud/quiet) excerpt plays. List loud and quiet animals; move silently as the chosen animals when the recording repeats. March is Here Now by title and page only — echo text; choose dynamics for lion and lamb parts; body percussion once, then speech again. Homeroom: animal moves beside desks.

Activity. Use the book guide. Loud and quiet hands.

Close. Sign so–la–so–mi once, then walk.

Week 25 · Notating SML, A Kite and Instrument Assessment

We read rhythm cards with ta and ti-ti, notate so–mi–la on a three-line staff, move like a kite for contour and tempo, label ABA with A Kite, and assess unpitched listening on paper.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi–la. One rhythm-card speak.

Rhythm Cards · Notating So–Mi–La (book p. 74). Speak rhythm cards with ta and ti-ti; touch shoulders on each rest. Notating SML: when so and mi are around lines, la is in the space above so (review p. 63 idea). Echo short fragments, then notate. Challenge: notate the first four pitches of Bounce High (on this site). Homeroom: paper staffs on desks.

Kite movement · A Kite ABA (book pp. 74–75). Kite imagery for contour and fast/slow (title and page only — tell the story; students pantomime). A Kite by title and page only: speak then sing with bordun if you have one; three students invent wind sounds (chimes, drum scrape, glissando); sit for song and move for wind to feel same/different/same; choose shapes and discover ABA; point to a simple song/wind/song chart. Homeroom: wind sounds with voices or one small instrument.

Which Instrument Do You Hear? · Written Assessment (book p. 76). Quick review of hidden unpitched sounds on the chart (see also p. 70). Then Written Assessment #3 by title and page only — or a sheet you draw with six icons: you play one hidden instrument; students circle the match. Homeroom: icons under the document camera.

Activity. Use the book guide. Contour and ABA.

Close. Sit–move–sit once for ABA, then walk.

Week 26 · Jack and Jill, Contour and Instrument Families

We copy Irish Washerwoman moves, trace contour cards with voices, sing and move Jack and Jill in ABA with a chord bordun, classify unpitched instruments as wood, metal, or skin in One, Two, Three, and form shamrock or clover groups from temple-block cues.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi–la. One contour siren.

Irish Washerwoman · Contour cards (book p. 77). Copycat moves while the jig plays — wash, wring, hang, sweep, stir (title and page only). Then present contour cards one at a time; class traces and matches vocal sounds; try one upside down. Homeroom: washer moves beside desks.

Jack and Jill ABA (book pp. 77–78). Title and page only. Trace contour on a chart; echo nonsense syllables then text; pat the beat and transfer to a chord bordun if you have bars; B section speech with boy/girl jump–crouch–sway actions; perform song/speech/song with an eight-beat introduction and name the form ABA. Homeroom: actions in two seated teams; bordun on one instrument or laps.

One, Two, Three · Shamrocks and Four-Leaf Clovers (book p. 79). One, Two, Three by title and page only: identify clapped rhythms on cards; speak the poem; label instrument families wood / metal / skin; players perform the poem rhythm by family. Shamrocks game: move to a drum pulse; freeze; temple blocks play three or four sounds; silent groups of three or four. Homeroom: march in place; form groups in the carpet rows.

Activity. Use the book guide. Instrument families.

Close. Show wood, metal, or skin with fingers, then walk.

Week 27 · Acka Backa and Bunnies on the Move

We read rhythm cards that hide rests, build Acka Backa contour with movable word cards and so–mi–la, send players to a chord bordun on “you,” and begin Bunnies on the Move with quiet same/different moves. Book note: shift this week to land before Easter if needed.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi–la. One soft bunny freeze.

Rhythm Cards with rests (book p. 80). Read cards from p. 56 idea. Extension: speak a card aloud only if it has a rest somewhere; if there is no rest, internalize the whole card silently. Homeroom: cards under the document camera.

Acka Backa contour game (book pp. 80–81). Acka Backa is on this site. Affix word cards on the board so they can move. Speak four cards at a time; sing all on so while pointing; then sing the SML melody and slide cards up or down to show contour; add hand signs; pat the beat. Game: seated circle; teacher points on each beat; the child on “you” moves to play the chord bordun; rotate. Homeroom: one instrument; waiting students pat the bordun on laps.

Bunnies on the Move! (book p. 81). Title and page only — scan ends mid-activity (p. 82 not in the March PDF). Echo the bunny text; keep voices quiet; explore different quiet locomotor or nonlocomotor moves while temple blocks (or two woods) cue change. Same/different idea: freeze in a new shape when the pattern changes. Homeroom: quiet tip-toe beside desks. Teacher note from the book: no new objectives — you may slide this week to the week before Easter and shift later lessons forward one week.

Activity. Use the book guide. Quiet bunny moves.

Close. Quiet tip-toe four beats, then walk.
