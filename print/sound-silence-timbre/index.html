Sound and silence, rests in the body, wood/metal/skin, and same/different.

Week 5 · Sound and Silence

Music has sounds and silences. A silent beat still counts: we freeze, keep the pulse inside, and come back in together.

Warm-up. Whisper, talk, sing “sound.” Then say nothing for one beat: that is silence.

Sound and silence boxes. Put up 4 boxes; leave one empty. Pat on the full boxes, open hands (freeze) on the empty one. Move the empty box and try again.

Read-aloud · The Listening Walk. Use the book guide. Echo the sound words and freeze on the quiet hand.

Read the boxes. A child moves the empty box. The class pats and freezes to match.

Activity. Chant Pease Porridge Hot. On each silent beat, freeze hands in the air.

Close. Drum pulse; freeze on the silent beat at the door.

Week 6 · Rest in the Body

A silent beat is a rest. We show rests with our bodies and play games where the rest matters.

Warm-up. Buzz high and low like a bee, then silence.

Bee Bee Bumblebee. Learn it by echo. Pat the pulse; on each rest after “bee” and “knee,” hands open wide.

Read-aloud · The Quiet Book. Use the book guide. Pat three, rest on four before each page.

Bow Wow Wow partners. Partners face each other: stamp, stamp, stamp on “bow wow wow,” shake a finger on “whose dog,” clap hands, and jump to face a new partner on the rest.

Activity. Sit in a circle. The leader points to one child per beat. Whoever gets “out!” becomes the next leader. Rests are open hands.

Close. Walk to the door; freeze on the rest.

Week 7 · Wood, Metal, Skin

Instruments are made of different things. Wood, metal and skin (drum) each have their own sound colour.

Warm-up. Say “wood” on the woodblock sound, “metal” on the triangle, “skin” on the drum.

Three families. Show each instrument. Tap it on the pulse. Name the material. Put it on its mat.

Read-aloud · My Family Plays Music. Use the book guide. Hold up each instrument and name its family.

Sort objects. Tap classroom objects gently. Is it wood, metal or other? Sort onto mats.

Activity. Eyes closed. Play one. Eyes open: point to the mat.

Close. Line up to the woodblock pulse.

Week 8 · Same and Different

Music can be the same or different. We hear it, move it, and stand on the matching shape.

Warm-up. Clap 4 beats; the class copies. Same!

Same or different?. Play two short drum patterns. Same: thumbs up. Different: wiggle fingers.

Read-aloud · From Head to Toe. Use the book guide. Each move is done four times on the pulse.

Stand on the shape. Circle = part 1 sound (woodblock), square = part 2 (drum). Walk the pulse; stand on the matching shape when the sound changes.

Activity. Soft pulse = tiptoe. Strong pulse = march. When the music changes, change your move.

Close. Tiptoe to the door.
