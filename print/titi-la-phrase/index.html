Ti-ti, La & Phrases

Ti-ti on rhythm cards, so–mi staff and Valentine, Drummer Hoff ostinato and patriotic songs, then label la with Bounce High, light mallets, and unpitched listening.

Week 20 · Which Rhythm and Notating So–Mi

We hear and name four-beat cards that mix ta, ti-ti, and rest, notate so and mi on a three-line staff, sing Oliver Twist with same/different shapes, add sticks to Cumberland Square, and explore villains, damsels, and heroes with Melodrama voices.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi. One short ta/ti-ti clap echo.

Which Rhythm Do You Hear? (book p. 59). Post five four-beat cards with ta, ti-ti, and rest (title and page only — draw your own). Class speaks and claps each card. You clap one; students show the card number with fingers. Homeroom: cards under the document camera.

Notating SM (book p. 59). Three-line staff on the board: middle line is So; Mi is on the line below. Class sings short SSM examples with hand signs; soloists echo. Review Engine, Engine (on this site) and discover the four parts are the same so–so–mi–mi. Homeroom: board or magnetic boards.

Oliver Twist · Cumberland Square · Melodrama (book pp. 60–61). Oliver Twist by title and page only — speak with pulse actions, add melody, sing/think/sing, show three-part form with shape cards (same shape for repeated parts). Cumberland Square with sticks: Part One touches body parts; Part Two taps sticks (Ti-ti ta feel). Melodrama: villain / damsel / hero voices with accordion-folded paper napkins. Homeroom: sticks beside desks; Melodrama seated.

Activity. Use the book guide. Ta and ti-ti names.

Close. Speak one rhythm card once, then walk.

Week 21 · Shapes, Valentine and I Hope!

We move with same/different shapes, play so–so–mi Valentine on a barred instrument, notate so–mi with partners, read I Hope! with a mail-carrier drum game, and hide Lucy Locket’s pocket with kind loud and quiet.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi. One shape freeze.

Moving with Shapes (book p. 62). Class assigns a locomotor move to each shape (march, gallop, tip-toe…). Hold up a shape; class moves and freezes when it goes down. Homeroom: small steps beside desks.

Valentine / SSM on bars (book p. 62). Review Teddy Bear with so–so–mi signs; substitute “Valentine.” Demonstrate S–S–M (G–G–E) on a vertical glockenspiel (only G and E if you can). Half play SSM; half do actions. Homeroom kit: one carried-in glock or xylo; short turns.

Notating So–Mi · I Hope! · Lucy Locket (book pp. 63–64). Pairs notate SSM/SMS on three-line staff cards (melody moves left to right). I Hope! by title and page only — mail carrier delivers a numbered Valentine card; recipient plays that rhythm on conga or hand drum (alternate hands). Lucy Locket (on this site): hide the pocket; sing quieter when far and louder when near — loud is not a shout. Homeroom: one drum in the circle.

Activity. Use the book guide. Kind deliveries.

Close. Quiet tip-toe to the door on a triangle shape.

Week 22 · Ostinato, Presidents and Patriotic Songs

We freeze to a march, speak a Drummer Hoff ostinato on hand drums, cheer Celebrate the Presidents! with ta and ti-ti cards, stand for O Canada, and add actions to She’ll Be Comin’ ’Round the Mountain.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi. One move-and-freeze.

Move and Freeze · Drummer Hoff (book pp. 65–66). Scattered move when the march plays; freeze when it stops — explore levels and directions. Drummer Hoff by title and page only: speak the ostinato, transfer to hand drums with mallets, quiet tremolo on “Ready… Aim… Fire!” and one accent on “Kahbahblooom!” Dramatize with a few actors. Homeroom: drums in a small rotation; soft mallets.

Celebrate the Presidents! (book p. 66). Title and page only. March while you echo the poem; build a four-beat ostinato from Lincoln (ta ta) and Washington (ti-ti ti-ti) cards; speak it four times with cheer actions; return to the poem. Homeroom: march in place beside desks.

Patriotic Sing · She’ll Be Comin’ ’Round the Mountain (book p. 67). Book lists Yankee Doodle and America by title and page — use O Canada (on this site) for our repertoire; stand respectfully if you play a recording. She’ll Be Comin’ by title and page only — add verse actions; reverse actions at the end of the fourth phrase. Homeroom: actions in place.

Activity. Use the book guide. Ostinato and accent.

Close. March four beats in place, then walk.

Week 23 · Label La, Mallets and Miss Mary Mack

We review phrase-length body patterns, bounce to prepare la, give la its name and Curwen sign, copy mallet ideas in Do As I’m Doing, guess hidden unpitched instruments, and clap Miss Mary Mack with a partner pulse.

Warm-up. Greet on so–mi. Reach once for a high la siren (soft).

Phrase echoes · Bounce High · Labeling La (book p. 68). Short phrase-length body-percussion echoes (book p. 38 idea). Bounce High, Bounce Low on this site — bounce on the pulse. Label La: name the pitch above so, show the drooping-hand Curwen sign, echo the book’s call-and-response sequence (title and page only), then combine so–mi–la in four-beat patterns with signs.

Do As I’m Doing (book p. 69). Title and page only. Sing while marching; copy nonlocomotor moves; then lines behind barred instruments copy the teacher’s light mallet ideas (high/low, tremolo, glissando). Rotate the front player. Homeroom: one instrument; pencil mallets on desks for waiting students.

Which Instrument Do You Hear? · Miss Mary Mack (book p. 70). Hide six unpitched instruments; one child plays while another points to the matching icon. Miss Mary Mack by title and page only — echo verses; clap/out pulse ostinato; partner claps when ready. Homeroom: instruments behind a screen or under a cloth.

Activity. Use the book guide. Careful listening.

Close. Sign so–la–so–mi once, then walk.
