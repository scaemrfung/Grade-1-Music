Unit 6 · Print pack

Ti-ti, La & Phrases

Ti-ti felt and read, la joins so and mi, and phrases named with call and response.

Week 21 · Ti-ti: Two Sounds on a Beat

Some beats have two short sounds. We feel them in names and words before we write them.

Warm-up. Clap names: “Sam” (one), “Ma-ya” (two).

One or two?. Pat the pulse and say names. One sound on the beat or two?

Read-aloud · Tanka Tanka Skunk!. Use the book guide.

Name ti-ti. Now name two sounds on a beat: ti-ti.

Activity. Each child claps their name on the pulse; the class echoes.

Close. Line up saying names.

Week 22 · Ti-ti and Ta

We read and play ti-ti and ta in songs like Lucy Locket and Star Light.

Warm-up. Echo rows with ta and ti-ti.

Read ti-ti. Show ti-ti (two joined notes). Read rows: ta ti-ti ta ta.

Read-aloud · Charlie Parker Played Be Bop. Use the book guide.

Build a row. Pairs build rows with ta and ti-ti, then clap.

Activity. Sing it; clap the words and say ta or ti-ti.

Close. Line up.

Week 23 · Add La

A new note joins so and mi: la, just above so. We sign it and sing it.

Warm-up. Sign See-Saw.

Meet la. La sits just above so. Sign it (drooping hand). Sing so–la–so.

Read-aloud · Up, Up, Down. Use the book guide.

La on the staff. Place la in the space above so.

Activity. Sing Bounce High with signs.

Close. Line up.

Week 24 · Phrases Have Names

A phrase is a musical sentence. We name phrases, mirror them with a partner, and try call and response.

Warm-up. Rain, Rain with rainbows.

Name the phrase. “Each rainbow is a phrase.” Count phrases in Rain, Rain.

Read-aloud · Rain, Rain, Go Away!. Use the book guide.

Child caller. A child sings the call; the class answers.

Activity. Partners face each other; A leads a move for phrase 1, B copies; swap for phrase 2.

Close. Line up.
