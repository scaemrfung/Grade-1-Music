Mallets, Bordun & Reading

Mallet manners, a two-drum bordun, so–mi on the staff, and four-beat reading.

Week 17 · Mallet Manners

Mallets need manners. Rest, Ready, Play, Freeze: we learn to hold a mallet and let it bounce off the bar.

Warm-up. Wiggle fingers, make a “bird beak” grip on a pencil.

Mallet manners. Show the four cards: Rest (mallets down), Ready (mallets up, still), Play (bounce like a hot stove), Freeze. Practise with pencils on knees.

Read-aloud · Mole Music. Use the book guide.

Play See-Saw. Children take turns bouncing G–E on the xylophone for See-Saw; the class pretend-plays.

Activity. Two children at the xylophone bounce on the pulse; the class pretend-plays with pencils. Rotate.

Close. Line up.

Week 18 · Bordun and Pulse

A bordun is a steady low pattern under a song. We make one with two drums playing together on the pulse.

Warm-up. Left hand pats low (knee), right hand pats high (shoulder), together.

Two drums together. Play both drums at once on the pulse. That steady pattern is a bordun, like the floor of the song.

Read-aloud · Jam, Too?. Use the book guide. Layer instruments over the drum pulse.

Bordun on bars. Play C and G together on the xylophone (a bar bordun).

Activity. Two children play the bordun; the class sings See-Saw. Rotate.

Close. Line up.

Week 19 · So and Mi Get Names

High and low notes now have names: so and mi. We sign them, sing them, and see them on a three-line staff.

Warm-up. High and low slides.

So and mi. Show the so sign (flat hand, higher) and mi sign (flat hand, lower). Sing See-Saw with signs.

Read-aloud · Do Re Mi. Use the book guide.

Write it. A child places so and mi on the staff; the class sings it.

Activity. The teacher signs; the class sings so or mi.

Close. Line up.

Week 20 · Reading Four Beats

The heart rows become rhythm rows. We read four-beat rows of ta and rest.

Warm-up. Echo ta/rest rows.

Hearts become rows. Put the 4 hearts back up. Replace them with ta and rest cards. Read the row.

Read-aloud · The Jazz Fly. Use the book guide.

Row relay. Pairs read a row, then pass the cards on.

Activity. The teacher swaps one card; the class reads the new row.

Close. Line up.
