Week 9 · Ta: The Pulse Has a Name

The heartbeat pulse now gets a name: ta. We say ta, clap ta, and read hearts as ta.

Warm-up. Whisper, talk, sing “ta.”

Hearts become ta. Point to 4 hearts. “Each heart is one pulse. We call it ta.” Put a ta card under each heart. Say and clap.

Read-aloud · Chicka Chicka Boom Boom. Use the book guide. Half pats ta, half chants.

Play ta. Woodblock plays ta while the class claps. Then children take turns.

Activity. Sing Rain, Rain; a child points to the hearts on the pulse.

Close. Say “ta ta ta ta” as you walk.

This week in 3 classes (devotional song 5 min + music 20 min each).

Week 10 · Ta and Rest

We read and play ta and rest. A rest is a silent beat we can see on a card.

Warm-up. Clap 4 tas; the class echoes.

Meet the rest card. “Ta ta ta ___.” Show the rest card: a silent beat. Clap ta ta ta, open hands on rest.

Read-aloud · This Jazz Man. Use the book guide. Step or pat ta on every beat.

Play a row. Pairs play their row on drum or woodblock.

Activity. The teacher moves the rest card to a new box; the class claps and rests to match.

Close. Ta ta ta rest, walking.

This week in 3 classes (devotional song 5 min + music 20 min each).

Week 11 · Fast and Slow

Music can go fast or slow. That speed is called tempo. The pulse can change speed but it still stays steady.

Warm-up. Say “hello” slowly like a snail, then quickly like a cheetah.

Fast or slow?. Play the drum fast or slow. Walk to match. Freeze on the stop.

Read-aloud · We’re Going on a Bear Hunt. Use the book guide. Speed up for the run home.

Play the tempo. A child plays a fast or slow pulse on the woodblock; the class walks it.

Activity. Learn it by echo. Chug arms on the pulse.

Close. Slow walk to the door.

Week 12 · Melody Shape: Contour

A melody has a shape. It goes up, down, or stays the same. We draw it in the air and on the board.

Warm-up. Slide up, down, then stay on one sound.

Draw the tune. Sing See-Saw; draw each note in the air. Then draw the shape on the board.

Read-aloud · Little Melba and Her Big Trombone. Use the book guide. Trace sounds in the air.

Match the drawing. Three shapes on the board (up, down, zigzag). Hum one; children point.

Activity. Hold the yarn ball high or low as the tune goes up and down. Pass it on.

Close. Line up on the pulse.
